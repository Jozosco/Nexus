Report Highlights:
For marketing year (MY) 2012/13 post forecasts planted area for soybeans at 950,000 hectares. Post
estimates domestic crush for soybeans to increase dramatically due to national biofuels mandate aided
by steady demand for meal for feed use. Crush is estimated at 200,000 tons for MY2012/13, at 80,000
tons for MY2011/12 and at 60,000 tons for MY2010/11. Production for MY2011/12 is lowered to 1.6
million metric tons, based on lost area and lower than average yields as a result of extremely hot and
dry days in December and January.
