Report Highlights:
The GOR expanded the list of agricultural goods excluded from the free trade regime applicable in the
Kaliningrad Region to include palm, coconut, palm kernel, and babasu oils and their fractions, animal
or vegetable fats and oils and their fractions. The measure came into effect on March 22, 2012.
General Information:
On March 7, 2012, the GOR issued Decree No. 200 “On Amendments to Decree of the Government of
the Russian Federation of March 31, 2006, No. 186,” which expanded the list of agricultural goods
excluded from the free trade regime applicable in the Kaliningrad Region to include palm, coconut,
palm kernel, and babasu oils and their fractions, animal or vegetable fats and oils and their fractions.
The measure came into effect on March 22, 2012.
