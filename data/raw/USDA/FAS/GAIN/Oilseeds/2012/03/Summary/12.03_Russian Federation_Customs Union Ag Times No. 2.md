The Presidents of the Russia-Kazakhstan-Belarus Customs Union (CU) and the CU supreme regulatory
body, the Eurasian Economic Commission (EEC), met in Moscow on March 19, 2012. Summaries of
relevant developments and decisions from these meetings and regular weekly meetings of the EEC
working body, the EEC Collegium, that impact CU food and agriculture policy are contained in this
report. In particular, one of the decisions implements a CU import tariff raise for tropical oils effective
late April 2012.
