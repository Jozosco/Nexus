Report Highlights:
On August 22, 2012, the date of Russia’s WTO accession, as was prescribed by the Decision of the
Customs Union Commission No. 810 of September 23, 2011, the Russian Federal Service for
Veterinary and Phytosanitary Surveillance (VPSS) stopped requiring veterinary certificates for some
imported products, including grains for feed and feeds of plant origin such as soybean meal and
Distiller’s Dried Grains (DDGs). This measure is expected to help facilitate imports of feeds necessary
for the development of Russia’s livestock and poultry industries, and is expected to allow for greater
shipments from the United States of some of these products.
