Report Highlights:
On August 2, the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) issued a forecast predicting below-normal
(less than 94% of Long Period Average) monsoon rains for second half of the monsoon season (August-
September). Based on current outlook and rainfall distribution till date, seasonal rainfall for the entire
monsoon season is likely to be deficient (below 90% of LPA). With the kharif crop planting down 11
percent at 66 million hectares, the major crops likely to be affected by deficit rains are sugarcane,
cotton, rice, peanut, pulses and coarse grains.
