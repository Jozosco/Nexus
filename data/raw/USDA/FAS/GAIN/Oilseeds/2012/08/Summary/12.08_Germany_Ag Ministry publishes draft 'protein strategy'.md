Report Highlights:
Efforts by the German Green party to end soybean imports are beginning to draw more mainstream political
action. In June, the German Ministry of Food, Agriculture and Consumer Protection (BMELV) published a draft
‘protein strategy’ that calls for, among other things, increased legume production as a substitute for imported
soybeans. At the national policy level, the focus is mainly on legume crop research. For the EU’s Common
Market Policy (CAP), to which Germany is the largest contributor, there is a desire to encourage the domestic
production of protein crops as part of the CAP 2013 legislation. This report contains a summary translation of
BMELV’s protein strategy and a final version is expected to be published this fall.
