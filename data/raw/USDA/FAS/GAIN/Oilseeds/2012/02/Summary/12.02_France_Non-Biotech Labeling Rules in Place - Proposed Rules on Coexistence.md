Report Highlights:
France has recently published two new regulations concerning agricultural biotechnology. The first was
published in the Official Journal defining "non-biotech" labeling, and the second creates new rules for
the “coexistence” of cultivation of biotech crops near conventional crops. The labeling regulations
apply only to products produced in France, but not to imported products. The decree setting rules for
biotech coexistence appears to apply surprisingly little constraints on farmers.
