Approved By:
Bob Hoff, Agricultural Counselor
Prepared By:
Jeff Zimmerman, Agricultural Attaché
Report Highlights:
Post now forecasts soybean production in 2011/12 at 70 million metric tons (mmt), a significant
decrease of 7 percent from the record 75.3 mmt produced in 2010/11. The 2011/12 crop has been
adversely affected by drought conditions in southern Brazil, a result of the La Nina weather
phenomenon. The 2011/12 harvest has advanced quickly in recent weeks reaching over 25 percent of
planted area harvested. Exports in 2011/12 are now forecast to at 33 mmt, slightly less than the 33.8
mmt of exports in 2010/11.
