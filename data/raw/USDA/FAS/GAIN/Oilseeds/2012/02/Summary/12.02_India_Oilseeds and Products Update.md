Report Highlights:
Poor winter rains and lower oilseed area in Rajasthan have not only reduced rabi (winter sown)
oilseed planting to 8.1 million hectares, but also reduced the net availability of oilseeds for crushing
into edible oils. Total edible oil imports through Marketing Year (MY) 2011/12 will rise 11 percent
to 9.1 million tons on strong domestic demand. Recent volatility in exchange rate has cut the profit
margins of oil meal exporters. Considering strong domestic demand for oil meals, total oil meal
exports will drop 8 percent to 5.3 million tons.

New Delhi
