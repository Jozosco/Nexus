imports of soybeans continued their steady rise that began in 2009 with the opening of the sole oilseed crushing
plant in the country. In 2011, Tunisian soybean imports were estimated at about 490,000 MT, up from 320,000
MT in the year before. Soybean meal imports continued their decline, with a 13 percent drop in 2011 that
followed a 59 percent fall in 2010. Tunisia’s olive oil exports in 2011 totaled 100,000 MT, valued at $287
million, compared with $310 million in the previous year. U.S. corn oil exports increased by 62 percent in 2011,
reaching $110 million, and controlled 57 percent market share.
