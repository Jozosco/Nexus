Executive Summary:
Production
The Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) reported that total crude palm oil (CPO) production rose 5
percent to 1.3 million tons in April 2012. Peninsular Malaysia’s production increased 7 percent, while
East Malaysia’s output was up 3 percent. However, compared to April 2011, output during April 2012
was 6.8 percent lower. After almost 24 months of growth in production, the palms are undergoing
biological stress leading to lower yields. Post expects CPO output to be about 4.8 million tons during
the Apr-Jun 2012, compared to 5 million tons in the Apr-Jun quarter previous year.
