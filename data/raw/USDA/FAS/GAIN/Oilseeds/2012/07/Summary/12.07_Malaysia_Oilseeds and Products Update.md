Report Highlights:
In May, Malaysia's crude palm oil (CPO) production rose 9 percent to 1.4 million tons. Though the
weather had contributed positively to May CPO production, the biological stress the palms suffered for
the last 24 months has caused lower yields. Palm oil exports rose marginally to 1.3 million tons in
April. With a higher palm oil output, the monthly average delivered CPO price declined to $968/ton in
June.
Post:
Kuala Lumpur
Executive Summary:
Production
The Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) reported that total crude palm oil (CPO) production rose 9
percent to 1.4 million tons in May 2012. Peninsular Malaysia’s production increased by 9 percent
during the month of May, while East Malaysia’s output was up 8 percent. However, compared to May
2011, output during May 2012 was 7.2 percent lower. Increased in production for the month of May
2012 was attributed to favorable weather but the biological stress started to take effect since February
2012 had lead to lower yields for year 2012. Post expects CPO output to be about 4.8 million tons
during the Apr-Jun 2012, compared to 5.0 million in the Apr-Jun quarter previous year.
