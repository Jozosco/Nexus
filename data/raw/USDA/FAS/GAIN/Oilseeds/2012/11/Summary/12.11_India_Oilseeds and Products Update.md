Report Highlights:
Kharif season (fall harvested) soybean planted area is 4 percent higher than last year, and is
expected to produce a record 11.2 million tons of soybeans in marketing year (MY) 2012/13.
Winter oilseed planting is still in progress, and the latest Government of India report indicates that
the rapeseed-mustard has been planted on 3.7 million hectares, up 23 percent from MY 2011/12.
Adequate soil moisture from recent monsoon rains encouraged timely planting of winter oilseeds.
At the same time, the total availability of oilseeds for crushing is expected to decline, and will likely
push edible oil imports to a record 10.1 million tons. Increasing domestic food and feed demand for
oil meals will likely reduce exports to 5.4 million tons in MY 2012/13.
