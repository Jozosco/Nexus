Report Highlights:
Dry weather conditions throughout the winter and mild winter and spring temperatures, along with
abundant spring precipitation have delayed grain crop development in Spain. Spain’s total grain
production is projected to reach over average levels in MY2018/19, with harvest operations starting
later than usual. The strong demand by the export-oriented domestic livestock industry ensures the
continuation of the steady pace of grain imports, despite the sizeable crop and the ample pasture
supplies. Competitively priced U.S. sorghum has helped Spanish feed compounders’ in the transition
until new domestic crop became available.
