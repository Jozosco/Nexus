Report Highlights:
Post expects slower growth in the vegetable oil and oil meal sectors in MY 2018/19, given potential
declines in consumer purchasing power and general economic uncertainty. Mexican oilseed crushers
are expected to continue increasing oil production to keep up with population growth and meal demand
from the growing livestock sector, but there should be no expansion in production capacity. Mexico
remains very dependent on imported oilseeds and oilseed products, primarily soy from the United States
and rapeseed (canola) from Canada.
