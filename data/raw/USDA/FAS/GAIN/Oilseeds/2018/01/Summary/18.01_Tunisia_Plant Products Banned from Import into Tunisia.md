Report Highlights:
Tunisia bans the import of several plants and plant products from some or all countries, including but
not limited to citrus, dates, apples, pears, alfalfa, clover, potatoes, peaches, and olives.
Plant Products Banned from Import into Tunisia
A Tunisian Ministry of Agriculture Order dated June 6, 2015, bans the import of the following plants
and plant products thereof:
 Citrus and Dates from all countries
 Apples and Pears from all countries with Erwinia amylovora (Fire blight)
 Alfalfa, Clover, and Potatoes from all countries with Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. albedinis
(Bayoud Disease)
 Peaches from all countries with Bactrocera spp.
 Olives from all countries with Xylella fastidiosa.
Tunisia published the phytosanitary measure in the Journal Officiel de la République Tunisienne — July
10, 2015, N° 55, Page 1513-1514, which is available online. The measure is not available
