Report Highlights:
Bulgaria’s rapeseed harvest commenced earlier than normal this year. About two percent of rapeseed
area was harvested as of June 22. Northern production regions experienced less-favorable weather
conditions and yields will likely be negatively affected as a result. Average yields in southern Bulgaria
are likely to exceed those from marketing year (MY) 2017/18. The overall rapeseed harvested area is
13 percent higher than last year and despite inconsistent yields from region to region, Post forecasts an
eight-percent production increase over MY2017/18. Sunflower acreage declined sharply this year,
although to date, the current sunflower crop has developed well due favorable June weather. Forecast
yields are above last year and above average.
MY2017/18 rapeseeds ending stocks will be reduced due to active exports. Bulgaria’s sunflower crush
has increased significantly and exports of sunflower meal and oil during the first half of the marketing
year saw double digit growth over last year. MY2017/18 soybean meal imports increased by 12 percent
due to favorable demand from the poultry and pork industries.
