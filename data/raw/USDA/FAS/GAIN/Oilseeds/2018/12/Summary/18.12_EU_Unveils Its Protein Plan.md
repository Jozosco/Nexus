Report Highlights:
On November 22, the EU published its report on “The Development of Plant Proteins in the European
Union.” This document is a result of a yearlong stakeholder and expert consultation. It comes after calls
from the European Parliament and the Council to have a European Protein Plan. The EU is indeed
dependent on imports for its protein needs, as it imports approximately 75 percent of its consumption.
The growth of the EU-grown protein sector is seen as an opportunity for reduced dependency on
imports, and for European farmers to answer consumer demands for European-grown commodities.
This GAIN report highlights excerpts from the plant proteins report. It also relays insights from the
November 21-22 conference in Vienna, Austria “The Development of Plant Proteins in the European
Union - Opportunities and Challenges, including reactions to the judgement by the Court of Justice of
the European Union regulating new plant breeding techniques as Genetically Modified Organisms
(GMOs).
