Report Highlights:
Fall drought conditions led to a three-percent decline in winter rapeseed area planted and could reduce
the yield potential for winter rapeseed in marketing year (MY) 2019/20. Bulgarian farmers harvested
another good sunflower crop due to favorable weather, with average yields up 13 percent over 2017.
However, production is estimated to decline from the previous MY due to a lower area harvested.
Bulgaria is expected to remain a growing exporter of de-hulled sunflower seeds, sunflower meal, and
sunflower oil. Soybean meal imports are forecast to increase in MY2018/19.
General Information:
Weather Overview
