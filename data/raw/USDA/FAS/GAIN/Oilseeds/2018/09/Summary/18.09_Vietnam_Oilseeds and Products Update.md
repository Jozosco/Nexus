Report Highlights:
Post revises its forecast for soybean imports for marketing year (MY) 2018/19 (calendar year [CY]
2019) at 2.0 million metric tons (MMT), a significant increase from the MY2017/18 estimate of 1.75
MMT, as a result of strong feed demand in the livestock and aquaculture sectors and falling domestics
soybean planting areas. The United States had the largest market share for soybeans due to competitive
prices during the first six months of MY2017/18.
Post retains its projections for total soybean meal (SBM) imports at 5.15 MMT and 5.3 MMT in
MY2017/18 and MY2018/19, respectively, driven by the continued expansion of the domestic feed and
food industries.
1
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Vietnam soybean production has decreased over recent years due to low yields and a continuing decline
in growing area as farmers switch to more profitable crops, including other field crops and fruits and
vegetables. Soybean production continues to fall well below the demand of the food, livestock, and
aquaculture feed sectors.
