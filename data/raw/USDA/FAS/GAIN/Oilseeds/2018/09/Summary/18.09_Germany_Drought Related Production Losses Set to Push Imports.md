Report Highlights:
The drought has heavily affected grain, rapeseed, and forage production in Germany. As a result,
Germany is expected to strongly increase imports of soybeans/soybean meal and to become a net grain
importer. The US is set to become top source of soybeans to Germany. The drought was officially
declared, “a weather incident of national extent,” enabling the Government to award financial support
for over 10,000 farms in Germany.
