Report Highlights:
South Africa produced a historical-high summer oilseed crop of 2.5 million tons in the 2017/18 MY
with a further increase in oilseed production projected for the 2018/19 MY to 2.6 million tons. South
Africa demonstrated a positive trend in oilseeds plantings over the past 10 years, mainly driven by
investment in soybean processing capacity. As a result, oilseed meal imports are expected to drop from
contributing 70 percent to local consumption, 10 years ago, to contributing less than 20 percent to local
consumption in the 2018/19 MY.
