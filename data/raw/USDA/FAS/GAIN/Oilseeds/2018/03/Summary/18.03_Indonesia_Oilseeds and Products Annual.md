Report Highlights:
Palm oil production is expected to increase from 38.5 million tons in 2017/18 to 40.5 million tons in
2018/19. Exports and stocks are forecast to increase. Harvested area is revised for 2008-2017 based on
analysis of seed sales and seed trade data. Soybean imports are forecast up slightly to 2.85 million tons
for 2018/19.
Commodities:
Oil, palm
Production
Based on expectations for trend yield growth and revised area, 2018/2019 palm oil production is forecast
at 40.5 million tons, a 5 percent increase from 2017/2018. Total harvested area for 2008 – 2017 was
revised based on seeds sales and seed trade data (Figures 1 and Table 1). Harvested area for 2018/2019
is forecast at 11.3 million hectares.
