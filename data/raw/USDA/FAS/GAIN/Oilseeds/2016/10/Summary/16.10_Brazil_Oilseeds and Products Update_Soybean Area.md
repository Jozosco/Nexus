Report Highlights:
A record Brazilian soybean crop of 101 million metric tons (mmt) is forecast for the 2016/17 Marketing
Year (MY) as a result of a slight increase in planted area. Brazil is forecast to have the slowest area
growth in the last five years, attributed to an higher expected cost of production, tight credit policies,
higher interest rates, the economic downturn, and soybean areas shifting back to full-season corn.
Crush for the 2016/17 MY is forecast at 41 mmt to meet the increased biodiesel mandate and demands
from the animal feed sector. Post forecasts exports at a record of 57 mmt for 2016/17 MY due to the
weaker Brazilian Real and higher Chinese demand.
