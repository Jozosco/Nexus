Report Highlights:
The Government of India’s (GOI) Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) postponed
additional labeling requirements for pre-packaged foods, edible oils, and fats, until July 1, 2016.
General Information:
On December 31, 2015, FSSAI issued a notification to postpone the enforcement date for additional
labeling requirements for pre-packaged food, edible oils, and fats. The new deadline for compliance is
July 1, 2016. Previously, FSSAI had announced the enforcement date as December 31, 2015. For more
details, please refer to GAIN IN5013.
FSSAI’s notice is attached at the end of this report and can also be accessed on their website:
http://www.fssai.gov.in/.
