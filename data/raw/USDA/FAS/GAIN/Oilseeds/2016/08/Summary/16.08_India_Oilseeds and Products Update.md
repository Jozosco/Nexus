Report Highlights:
Assuming normal growing conditions through September, soybean production in marketing year (MY)
2016/17 (Oct-Sept) is estimated at 11.5 million metric tons (MMT). The most recent planting report
from the Ministry of Agriculture (MinAg) indicates that soybeans were planted on 11.3 million
hectares; future MinAg reports will likely reflect an increase in the area planted. Due to a return to the
normal monsoon rainfall, soybean yields are expected to increase to the 5-year average. The estimated
soybean oil imports in MY 2015/16 are raised to 4.3 MMT, which is the highest in the last decade. On
the contrary, oilmeal exports are estimated at a historic low of 450,000 metric tons.
