Report Highlights:
Planting of the key winter crops in Australia is just starting in some areas. The majority of cropping
regions are still waiting for rainfall to increase soil moisture levels, however some farmers are taking a
risk and dry-seeding on the expectation of rainfall in the coming weeks. This report covers planting
expectations for key winter crops plus provides some more in depth information about other crops
grown in Australia which are not covered in regular reporting.
Seasonal outlook
