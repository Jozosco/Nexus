three decades.

growing area of the provinces of Buenos Aires, Santa Fe and Cordoba. Soybean planting has picked
up and according to the Ministry of Agriculture, almost one-third of the crop is in the ground. FAS
Buenos Aires maintains its area estimate for marketing year (MY) 2013/14 at 20.5 million hectares.
