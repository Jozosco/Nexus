All-India cumulative rains until July 17 were 16 percent above normal since the onset of the monsoon.
Official forecasters expect heavy rains for the week ending July 26 and above normal rains for the
month of July. Good rains have accelerated planting and planted area has reached 61.2 million
hectares, 23 percent ahead of a year ago when a late monsoon delayed the onset of planting across much
of India.
