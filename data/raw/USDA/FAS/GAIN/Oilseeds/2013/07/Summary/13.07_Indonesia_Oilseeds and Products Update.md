 Minister of Trade Regulation No. 25 (Regulation No. 25) that aimed at providing price and
market guarantee for soybeans is unlikely to automatically trigger farmers to grow more
soybeans due to unfavorable weather conditions and revenue.
 Soybean shipments to Indonesia for October delivery could experience delays if Minister of
Trade Regulation No. 24/2013 (Regulation No. 24) is implemented as written.
 2011-2013 forest moratorium which has been extended to May 2015 and series of land use
disputes have not put a brake on oil palm area expansion in Indonesia. Indonesian oil palm area
is expected to reach 10.325 million hectares in 2014.
