Report Highlights:
MY 2013 Turkish soybean area and production are estimated about 30,000 hectares and 110,000 MT.
Low cotton returns helped to increase soybean planting. Turkey’s production of both poultry and
livestock are growing pushing the demand up for imported soybean and meal. Turkey imported a total
832,000 MT of soybeans during the first eight months of MY 2012 and US was the leading supplier
with 487,000 MT. Turkey also imported 560,000 MT of soy meal and US supplied 267,000 MT. The
GSM 102 credit guarantee program has helped US exports to Turkey. Total registration for both
products reached US$ 283 million so far during financial year of which US$ 160 million were for
soybeans and the rest US$ 123 million were for soybean meal. GOT canceled the controversial
biodiesel law which according to the law about one percent of biodiesel were going to be added to the
local diesel production starting from 2014.
