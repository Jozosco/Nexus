Report Highlights:
Favorable weather and strong MY 2012/13 oilseed prices encouraged farmers to plant an additional
300,000 hectares, bringing the total oilseed area to 8.1 million hectares. Edible oil imports are expected
to increase 11.3 percent to 10.1 million tons to meet rising demand for vegetable oils. During the same
period, total oil meal exports (not including rice bran and castor meal) are expected to drop 4 percent to
5.4 million tons as strong domestic demand for oil meal, coupled with increasing competition from
Latin America, will likely reduce Indian oil meal exports.
