Report Highlights:
The Government of Indonesia has eliminated import quotas, preshipment inspection, local soybean
purchase requirements, and government-mandated sales at set prices for soybeans. The Government
also has taken steps to temporarily remove the five percent duty on soybeans. These measures were
taken in order to combat dwindling local stocks and high soybean prices.
General Information:
Indonesia has made significant reforms to its soybean import policy in order to facilitate imports and
augment supply. Measures include eliminating:
 import quotas,
 pre-shipment inspection,
 local soybean purchase requirements,
 sales allocations to tempeh/tofu cooperatives at government-set prices.
