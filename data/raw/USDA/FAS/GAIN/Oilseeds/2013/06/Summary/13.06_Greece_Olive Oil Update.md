Report Highlights:
Greece is the third largest olive oil producer in the world behind Spain and Italy. According to industry
contacts, Greece’s MY 2012/13 (November/October) olive oil production climbed to approximately
350,000 MT thanks to favorable weather. More than 80 percent of the Greek annual production is extra
virgin olive oil. Per capita consumption of olive oil in Greece (20 Kg/year) is one of the highest in the
world. Ninety percent of Greek olive oil is exported to the European Union: 80 percent in bulk and 10
percent under Greek brand names.
