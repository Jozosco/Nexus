Report Highlights:
In Ukraine's Poltava region, FAS officials visited corn, oilseed, and wheat plots, and based on
observations and conversations with local agronomists, they appear to be progressing well. The
fields in this part of the country seemes to have received enough rainfall to continue healthy
growth. Some fields looked like they were planted later in the season, so the plant development
stages were lagging behind, but farmers did not express undo concern about this later start to the
growing season.
