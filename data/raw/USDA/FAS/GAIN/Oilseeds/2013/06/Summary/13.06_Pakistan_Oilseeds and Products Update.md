Report Highlights:
Cotton is Pakistan’s principal oilseed and sowing is lagging due to water shortages and a late
wheat harvest. Oilseed imports are on a downward trend due to relatively higher prices
compared to palm oil. During January - May 2013, Pakistan imported 266,269 metric tons (MT)
of oilseeds, a 35 percent decrease from the same period in 2012. Meanwhile, palm oil imports
for January- May 2013 increased 25 percent over the same period last year.
