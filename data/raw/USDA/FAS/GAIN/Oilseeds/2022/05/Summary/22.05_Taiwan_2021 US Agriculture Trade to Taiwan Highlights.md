Prepared By: Oscar Lin

Report Highlights:
In 2021, the total value of U.S. agricultural and related products exported to Taiwan was $3.94 billion,
an increase of 18 percent compared to the previous year. Taiwan moved up two places to rank as the 6th
largest market for U.S. agricultural exports in 2021. This was the second highest year on record behind
2018’s $4.08 billion. Products in the consumer oriented category continue to play an increasingly
important role, with beef and dairy products hitting new highs year after year. Taiwan is the largest
market for container shipments of U.S. soybeans ($736 million), but the ongoing shipping container
crisis is causing Taiwan buyers to increasingly favor competitors who can deliver in bulk shipments.
