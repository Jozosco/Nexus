3.5 million metric tons. FAS Cairo forecasts Egypt’s soybean, sunflower, and palm oil consumption for
food and industrial use in MY 2022/23 at about 2.65 MMT, up 4.7 percent compared to the MY 2021/22
volume of 2.53 million metric tons.
