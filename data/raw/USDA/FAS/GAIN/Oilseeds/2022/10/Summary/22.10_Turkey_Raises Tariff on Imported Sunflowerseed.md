Background:
According to a Presidential Decree published in the Official Gazette on October 20, 2022, Turkey
increased its tariff on sunflower seed from zero to 5 percent. The 5-percent tariff will be in effect from
October 27 through the end of December of this year. The tariff lines subject to this change fall under
Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) 1206.00.
Earlier this year, in an attempt to reduce domestic food inflation, the government zeroed-out import
tariffs on certain agricultural products, such as sunflowerseeds, meals, and oil. However, with the start
of the domestic sunflower harvest in September, the government has raised tariffs on imported
sunflowerseed and oil for the remainder of the year. The new tariffs remain below bound rates.
