Rice Prices Up Further
This week, the coarse rice price increased 4 percent over the previous week to BDT 55 (0.54) (Figure 1).
Farmers began the Aman season rice harvest in mid-November 2022. Usually, rice prices decline at
harvest time; however, high production costs are leading to high paddy prices and high rice prices.
Prices of fine and medium quality rice also rose slightly to BDT 75 ($0.74) and BDT 60 ($0.58) per
kilogram, respectively.
