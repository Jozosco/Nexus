Report Number: AR2022-0001
Report Name:Oilseeds and Products Update
Country: Argentina
Post: Buenos Aires
Report Category: Oilseeds and Products
Prepared By: Benjamin Boroughs
Approved By: Benjamin Boroughs
Report Highlights:
Despite initial favorable conditions, Argentine oilseed crops have faced warm and dry weather across
most growing regions since mid-December. Rains are needed soon to forestall more significant yield
reductions. Post reduces its projected Marketing Year (MY) 2021/22 soybean production to 46.5 million
metric tons (MMT), 3 MMT below USDA Official. More advanced drought conditions in Paraguay
could reduce Argentine imports below the current projected 4.5 MMT, which is 300,000 metric tons
below USDA Official. Argentine MY 2021/22 sunflowerseed and peanut production projections are
unchanged at 3.4 MMT and 1.3 MMT respectively.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
