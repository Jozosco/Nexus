Report Highlights:
Poland’s feed component markets – both grains and proteins, are on the rise. Stable demand for
vegetable proteins and weaker competition from South America provides for fertile ground for the U.S.
to significantly expand its share in the Polish soybean meal market in 2011.
General Information:
At the end of December 2010 the Ministry of Agriculture published the most recent data on average
industrial feed prices. November data shows a clear slowdown in the upward trend of rising prices
witness since June 2010. Despite the slowdown, prices for November exceeded respective prices for
May on various feed types by 9-22 percent. More information on current costs for feed and
consumption grains were published in the linked report:
http://gain.fas.usda.gov/Recent%20GAIN%20Publications/The%20New%20Expensive%20Year%20_
