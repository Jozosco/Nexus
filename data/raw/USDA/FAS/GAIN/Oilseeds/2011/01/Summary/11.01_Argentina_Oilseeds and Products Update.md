Report Highlights:
Widespread rainfall has provided relief to the oilseed crops in Argentina, however, hot and dry conditions still
prevail. For the marketing year 2010/11, soybean production is adjusted downward 1.5 million metric tons
(MMT) to 49 MMT, sunflower production remains unchanged and peanut area and production are raised to
265,000 hectares (up 40,000 hectares) and 850,000 MMT (up 70,000 tons), respectively.
