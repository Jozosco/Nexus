Report Highlights:
Post now forecasts record soybean production in 2011/12 at 75.5 million metric tons (mmt), a slight
increase over the 75.3 mmt produced in 2010/11. Brazil has experienced a record planting pace in
2011/12 with over 80 percent completed nationally. The concentrated planting of the estimated record
crop will greatly strain harvest and export logistics in 2012. Exports in 2011/12 are forecast to reach a
record 36 mmt, up 8 percent from an estimated 33 mmt of exports in 2010/11.
