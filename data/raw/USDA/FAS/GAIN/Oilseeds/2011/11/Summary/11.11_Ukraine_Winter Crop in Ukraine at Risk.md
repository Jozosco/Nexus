GAIN Report – UP1141 – Winter Crop Plantings in Ukraine at Risk Page 2 of 2
General Information:
According to recent reports by Ukraine’s Hydrometcenter, weather conditions for winter crops in
Ukraine were not favorable. Below average top soil moisture and colder than normal temperatures
resulted in uneven seed sprouting and winter crop development. Plants that did not have sufficient
resources to form strong sprouts and then a good root system will be going into the winter
underdeveloped and may not survive until spring.
