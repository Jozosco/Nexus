Report Highlights:
Harvest in Ukraine is close to completion with over 23 Million Metric Tons of wheat produced and 9.5 Million
Metric Tons of barley. Corn is being harvested and is expected to reach a record high number. Exports were slow
at the start of the new Marketing Year due to introduction of grain export duties. Domestic agricultural commodity
prices dropped down at the start of the Marketing year 2011/12 but are expected to go up in the near future.
Export duties on wheat and corn eliminated starting October 22, 2011, but for barley will remain in place until
January 2012.
