Report Highlights:
Malaysia's crude palm oil (CPO) production rose 2 percent to 1.9 million tons in October 2011. Palm
oil exports jumped 19 percent, resulting in a lower carry-out stock level at the end of October. Stronger
overseas demand pushed the monthly average delivered CPO price to $968/ton during the month of
November.
Post:
Kuala Lumpur
Executive Summary:
Production
The Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) reported that total crude palm oil (CPO) production rose 2
percent to 1.9 million tons in October 2011. Peninsular Malaysia’s production increased about one
percent during the month of October, while East Malaysia’s output was up 3.7 percent. Compared to
October 2010, output during October 2011 was 16.6 percent higher. The palms are expected to
experience a seasonal downtrend in output from November till February. Post still expects CPO output
to be about 5.1 million tons during the Oct-Dec quarter and the total CPO output for 2011 is expected to
touch 19 million tons.
Price
The monthly average delivered CPO price rose from $929/ton in October to $968/ton in November. For
comparison, the average CPO price for October 2010 was $1,029/ton.
