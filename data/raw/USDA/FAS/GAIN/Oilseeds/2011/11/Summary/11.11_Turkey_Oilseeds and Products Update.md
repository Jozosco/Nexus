Report Highlights:
MY 2010 imports of soybean reached 1.35 MMT, higher than early season estimates due to higher utilization in poultry and
dairy industries. Imports from US remained about 419,000 MT about 50 percent of the previous year mainly due to biotech
restrictions and buyers postponing purchases to the second half of the marketing year because of large carryover that
benefited South American sources. Soybean meal imports are up, reaching 534,000 MT. Soy oil imports however were
lower than expected due to price advantage of competing oils and new bio-safety law limiting utilization. FY 2011
utilization of the GSM-102 credit guarantee program was limited for US soybean and meal purchases due to the import
restrictions during the finalization of the new bio-safety law.
