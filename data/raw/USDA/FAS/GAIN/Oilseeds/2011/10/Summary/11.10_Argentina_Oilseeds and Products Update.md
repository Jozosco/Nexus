Report Highlights:
Soybean area for MY2011/12 is maintained at 19 million hectares, 300,000 ha lower than the official USDA
estimate. Soybean exports for MY2010/11 are bumped up by 1.2 million metric tons (MMT) to 10.5 MMT due
to the strong pace during the first six months of the year. Sunflower area and production for MY2011/12 are
expanded to 1.875 million hectares and 3.2 MMT respectively. Post maintains the estimate of sunflower crush
for MY2010/11 at 3.4 MMT (up 350,000 from USDA official numbers) due to a sunflowerseed harvest with
record yields and higher production than expected.
