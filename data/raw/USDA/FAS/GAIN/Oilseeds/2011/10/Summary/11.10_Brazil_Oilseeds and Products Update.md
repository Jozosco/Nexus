Report Highlights:
Post now forecasts soybean production in 2011/12 at 75 million metric tons (mmt), a slight decrease
from the record 75.3 mmt produced in 2010/11. Post forecasts a record 25 million hectares will be
planted to soybeans in 2011/12, a 3 percent increase over 2010/11 planted area of 24.2 million hectares.
The 2011/12 soybean crop has just commenced planting with over 5 percent planting concluded in the
highest and second highest producing states of Mato Grosso and Parana. Producers are heavily
investing windfall 2010/11 profits in increased use of certified seed, genetically engineered seed,
fertilizer, new machinery, soil management and other inputs. Post anticipates these investments to
result in very good national yields of 3 metric tons per hectare (mt/ha), despite expanded first-year
planted acreage that is expected to yield less than the average. Soybeans maintain a lower relative
production cost and greater liquidity compared to alternative crops. Exports in 2011/12 are expected to
