Report Highlights:
Peru's increasing demands for soybean meals and oil are largely attributed to country’s strong economic
performance. Soybean meal and oil imports are forecast at 1MMTand 380,000 MT, respectively for
MY2011. The Peruvian 480 million bird-per-year poultry sector is the main consumer for imported
soybean meal.
Executive Summary:
Soybean meal imports into Peru are forecast at 1 MMT in MY 2011, a slight increase of 15,000 MT compared to the
previous year. Poultry meat continues to be one the cheapest sources of protein in the Peruvian diet and the main users of
soybean meal, total poultry meat consumption was 860,000 MT in CY 2010. Soybean meal imports in CY 2010 were
985,273 MT of which 11 percent were imported from the United States. The recently approved Consumer Code includes
mandatory labeling for GM products, regulation to this Code is currently being drafted.
