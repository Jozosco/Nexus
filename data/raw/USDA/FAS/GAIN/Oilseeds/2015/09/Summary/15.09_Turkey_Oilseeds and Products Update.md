Report Highlights:
MY 2015 sunflowerseed area and production are now estimated at 505,000 hectares and 1 MMT,
respectively. Despite a good start due to rainy spring, lack of rains and extreme heat in the summer
months adversely affected yields. While sunflowerseed imports are projected to increase about 30
percent in MY 2015 to meet the local demand, sun meal imports are expected to increase about only six
percent due to possible increases in imports of alternative products such as DDGS. Sun oil imports
continued to be strong in MY 2014 due to mostly increasing exports to neighboring countries despite
the political turmoil in the export destinations.
