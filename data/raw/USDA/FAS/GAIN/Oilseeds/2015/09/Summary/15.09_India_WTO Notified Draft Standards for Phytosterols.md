On September15, 2015, India notified to the World Trade Organization (WTO) draft regulations which

The GOI’s Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) amended the Food Safety and
Standards (Food Products Standards and Additives) Regulations, 2011, and the Food Safety and
Standards (Packaging and Labeling) Regulations, 2011, to establish permissible limits and labeling
requirements for phytosterol as a food ingredient in certain foods. On September 15, 2015, India
notified these draft regulations to the WTO for comment (WTO Notification Number:
G/SPS/N/IND/117)
