Report Highlights:
Post revises down its marketing year (MY) 2015/16 (Oct-Sept) soybean production forecast from 12.5
million metric tons (MMT) to 11 MMT. Abnormal weather conditions will reduce yields and lead to an
overall production decline, despite higher area planted for kharif (fall harvest) oilseeds. Oilmeal exports
in MY 2015/16 will reach their second lowest point during the past 10 years (3.3 MMT). Softening
international vegetable oil prices will continue to encourage higher imports (14.5 MMT) of both soft
and palm oils, notwithstanding of India’s new five percent tariff hike for edible oils.
