Malaysia announces replanting incentives for oil palm plantation companies
The Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) announced on September 26, 2015, the allocation of $22.72
million (RM$100 million) for a replanting program geared to oil plantation companies for October 1-
December 31, 2015. This incentive will be based on a first-come, first-serve basis. An incentive of
$341 (RM$1,500) per ha will be disbursed for 33,000 ha, followed by another of $227 (RM$1,000) per
ha to be disbursed for 50,000 ha. Thus, total replanting areas targeted will be 83,000 hectares of
unproductive old palm plantations.
