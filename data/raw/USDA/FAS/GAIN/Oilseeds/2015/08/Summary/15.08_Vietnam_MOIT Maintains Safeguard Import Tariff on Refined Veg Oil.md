Report Highlights:
On August 10, 2015, the Vietnamese Government (GVN) released Decision 8287/QD-BCT regarding
the announcement of the midterm review of the application of official safeguard duties on refined soy
and palm oil imports. The safeguard measures increase the import tariff on refined soy and palm oil
products, aiming to help domestic refiners in response to an official request from domestic producers.
Decision 8287 states that additional import duties will be applied and lowered over time, falling to 0 on
May 8, 2017.
1
Policy:
In 2013, GVN applied an official safeguard measure against imported refined soy and palm oils
claiming that domestic refiners in Vietnam were negatively impacted by the growing volume of refined
oil imports, primarily from Malaysia and Indonesia. This safeguard is also applied again refined soy
and palm oil imports from the United States. Please see GAIN VM3051 for more details regarding the
application of the safeguard and GAIN VM5019 for information concerning Vietnam vegetable oil
production, consumption, trade, import tariffs.
