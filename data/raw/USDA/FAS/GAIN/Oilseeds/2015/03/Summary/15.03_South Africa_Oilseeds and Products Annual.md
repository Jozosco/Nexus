Report Highlights:
Oilseed production in South Africa is expected to decrease by 15 percent in the 2014/15 MY, due to a
mid-summer drought that hit the main summer rainfall producing areas. Post, however, forecasts that
oilseed production will increase in the 2015/16 MY to reach a historical high of 2.0 million tons on 1.5
million hectares, due to the added soybean crushing capacity and the increased affinity by farmers to
use soybeans as a rotational crop with corn. As a result, South Africa will crush a record of 1.8 million
tons of oilseeds in the 2015/16 MY and oilseed meal imports will drop by 15 percent to about 495,000
tons.
