Nicolas Rubio, Agricultural Attaché
Report Highlights:
FAS/Brasilia forecasts 2015/16 soybean production at 94 million metric tons (mmt) despite a drop in
planted area to 31.1 million hectares, a decrease of one percent compared to 2014/15. The drop in area
is attributed to the relative low global soybean prices, the economic challenges in Brazil, and the
expectation of higher interest rates. Soybean exports in marketing year 2015/16 are forecast to reach a
record 48 mmt, up four percent compared to the previous marketing year. This forecast is based on
strong demand by China and the expected weak Brazilian Real through 2015 and 2016.
SOYBEAN PRODUCTION
2015/2016 Outlook: Record Crop Forecast Despite Lower Planted Area
Post forecasts 2015/16 soybean production at 94 million metric tons (mmt), slightly higher than 2014/15
production estimates. The 2015/16 planted area is forecast to drop to 31.1 million hectares (ha), a
decrease of one percent compared to 2014/15. The drop in area is attributed to the relative low global
soybean prices, the economic challenges expected in Brazil, and higher interest rates.
