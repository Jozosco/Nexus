Report Highlights:
Over the last several years, the Bulgarian oilseeds industry has invested in expanding capacities,
diversifying and adding value to some of the major field crops. This trend has been most pronounced
with the sunflower crop. While until recently Bulgaria was a net exporter of sunflower seeds, lately the
country has increased its crushing capacity. The industry also invested in new processing businesses
such as production of bakery sunflower seeds for human consumption. In 2015 Bulgaria may emerge
as a producer of high oleic and confectionary sunflowers. As a result of these developments, Bulgaria
is on a trend to consume most of its sunflower crop and become a net exporter of processed products.
