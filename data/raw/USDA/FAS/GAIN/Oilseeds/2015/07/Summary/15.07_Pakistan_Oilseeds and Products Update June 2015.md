Report Highlights:
Production of cotton, far and away Pakistan’s largest domestic source of oilseeds, is expected to reach
10.0 million 480 lb bales, unchanged from the current USDA 2015/16 forecast. Planting is nearly
complete and slightly ahead of the year ago pace. Total area is expected to reach 3.15 million hectares,
200,000 hectares above the current USDA estimate. Imports of soybeans are expected to rise following
a recent decision by the Government of Pakistan to increase the tariff on soybean meal.
