Report Highlights:
Oilseeds and products in Ukraine are expected to be relatively more profitable than grains in 2015.
Farmers have expanded planting acreage, weather outlook remains positive, although economic
factors might impact yields and expectations. The expansion of domestic crush capacity will
stimulate the outflow of processed oil products both to domestic and international markets.
Ukraine’s oilseed sector will provide a positive boost to the national economy as a large earner of
foreign currency.
