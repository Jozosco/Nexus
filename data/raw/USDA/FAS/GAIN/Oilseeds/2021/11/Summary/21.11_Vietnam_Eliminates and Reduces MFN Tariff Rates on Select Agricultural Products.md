Report Highlights:
The Government of Vietnam (GVN) issued Decree 101/2021/ND-CP on November 15, 2021, revising
Most-Favored-Nation (MFN) tariff rates on corn, wheat, pork, and planting seeds. Vietnam will
eliminate the MFN duty for all classes of wheat and select planting seeds, lower the MFN corn tariff to
two percent, and lower the frozen pork tariff to ten percent. The corn, wheat, and planting seeds tariffs
will go into effect on the same date the Decree enters into force, December 30, 2021, while the lower
tariff rate for frozen pork will be effective on July 1, 2022.
