Required Report: Required - Public Distribution Date: November 01, 2021
Report Number: CH2021-0132
Report Name: Oilseeds and Products Update
Country: China - People's Republic of
Post: Beijing
Report Category: Oilseeds and Products
Prepared By: FAS China Staff
Approved By: Adam Branson
Report Highlights:
China’s soybean imports in marketing year (MY) 20/21 hit a record 99.8 million metric tons
(MMT) on high feed demand in the swine and poultry sectors. Soybean imports are expected to
reach 101 MMT in MY 21/22 on increasing demand for soybean meal and soybean oil and lower
imports of rapeseed year-over-year. U.S. share of China’s soybean imports reached 37.2 percent
in MY 20/21.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF AND NOT
NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Production
The Post forecast for MY 21/22 oilseed production remains unchanged at 62.9 MMT, 0.2 MT
lower than MY 20/21.
Soybeans
Post maintains MY 21/22 soybean production at 19 MMT, down from an estimated 19.6 MMT
in MY 20/21 reflecting a loss of planting area to corn production. The forecast production is
based on planted area at 9.6 million hectares (MHa) in MY 21/22, a projected decrease of over
2.7 percent compared to the previous year, and an average three-year yield of 1.97 MT/Ha. (See
more in August Oilseed Report).
