AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
In accordance to the news release (in Ukrainian) by the Ministry of Economic Development, Trade and
Agriculture (MEDTA), Ukraine sets an export cap for sunflower oil (HS Code 1215) at 5,380 TMT for
local marketing year (MY) 2020/21 (September 2020-August 2021).
This was done in a format of a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between MEDTA
and Ukroliyaprom, which is the industry association that represents interests of major oil crushers and
processors in Ukraine. This is similar to the MOUs that were employed to put caps on grain exports, for
more details see our GAIN Report UP2021-0006.
According to MOU’s copy available to Post, export caps are set before September 1, 2021, which
potentially opens a way for oil exporters to continue unobstructed exports of their stocks in September
before the standardized MY2020/21 (October 2020- September 2021) is concluded.
