Soybeans (Glycine max) can be classified into two distinct categories based on use: (i) food-grade, primarily used
for direct human consumption and (ii) feed-grade, primarily used for crushing and animal feed. In comparison to
feed-grade soybeans, food-grade soybeans used in Japan have a higher protein and sugar content, typically lower
yield and are not genetically engineered (GE). Japan is a key importer of both feed-grade and food-grade
soybeans (2020 Japan Oilseeds Annual).
