Health Canada Launches Consultation on Novel Foods Regulation Focused on Plant Breeding
On March 25, 2021, Health Canada launched a consultation on two new pieces of guidance for the Novel Foods
Regulations, focused on plant breeding. The consultation will be open for 60 days, from March 25 to May 24,
2021.
The two new pieces of guidance are attached. They are:
 Proposed Changes to Health Canada Guidance on the interpretation of Division 28 of Part B of the Food
and Drug Regulations (the Novel Food Regulations): When is a food that was derived from a plant
developed through breeding a “novel food”?
 Proposed Health Canada Guidance on the pre-market assessment of foods derived from
Retransformants under Division 28 of Part B
