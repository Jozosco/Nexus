Report Highlights:
On June 30, 2021, the Vietnam Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD) issued
Circular 05/2021 Promulgating the Amendment 1:2021 of National Technical Regulation (NTR) 190:
Maximum Allowable Levels (MLs) of Undesirable Substances in Animal Feed and Aqua Feed
Ingredients. This Amendment removes the zero tolerance for Salmonella and maximum levels for
arsenic, cadmium, lead, and mercury in feed ingredients of plant origin.
