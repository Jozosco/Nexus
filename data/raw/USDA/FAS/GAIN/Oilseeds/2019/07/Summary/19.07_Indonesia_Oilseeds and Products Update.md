Report Highlights:
The Government of Indonesia (GOI) is reviewing palm planted area following conflicting data from
several government agencies. Soybean imports for 2018/19 are forecast to reach 2.65 million tons
based on updated trade data. A target date of January 2020 has been set for implementing nationwide
B30 blending, a potential boon to domestic industrial CPO consumption.

Oil, palm
Production
Palm oil production for 2019/20 is unchanged at 43 million tons, an increase of 1.5 million tons from
2018/19 due to expanding mature area.
Recently, the Ministry of Agriculture’s (MOA) official data for planted area has been contradicted by
data from other government agencies. The discrepancy led the Government of Indonesia (GOI) to
announce in May 2019 a review and verification of palm area source data. Data generated using satellite
images suggest palm planted area is significantly larger than official MOA figures. The verification
process, which will include a multi-agency review, is expected to conclude in August 2019 and may
result in changes to planted area and overall yields.
