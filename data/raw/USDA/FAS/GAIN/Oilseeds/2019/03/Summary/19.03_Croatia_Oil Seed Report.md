Report Highlights:
This report contains marketing year (MY) 2019/20 forecast for Croatia’s production in soybean,
rapeseed, and sunflower and updated numbers for MY 2018/19.
General Information:
The Croatian livestock sector has not recovered from the decline that began just before Croatia’s EU
accession in 2013 due to uncompetitive prices. This decline deepened in 2014 due to the Russian
embargo on EU agro-food products and in 2015 due to the EU’s abolition of dairy quotas. Thus, soy oil
and cake production is decreasing sharply, while rapeseed and sunflower oil production are on the rise.
These trends are expected to continue.
