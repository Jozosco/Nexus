Report Highlights:
This report contains an unofficial translation of Law No. 2005-95 of October 18, 2005 on Animal
Husbandry and Animal Products. The law’s scope include imported animals, animal products, and
animal feed, but does not extend to aquaculture. Where known, the law’s relevant implementing
decrees and orders have been footnoted. Tunisian imports of products regulated by this law totals $600
million, including $10-40 million from the United States. Feed and feed ingredients account for over
two-thirds of current trade while market access for animal products remains limited by tariff and non-
tariff barriers.
