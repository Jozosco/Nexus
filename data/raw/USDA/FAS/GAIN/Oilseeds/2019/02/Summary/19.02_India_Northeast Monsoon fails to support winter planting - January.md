Report Highlights:
The cumulative rainfall for the Northeast Monsoon 2018 reported by the Indian Meteorological
Department (IMD), as of January 4, 2019 was forty-four percent lower than the fifty-year average. The
deficit rains have affected reservoir storage levels, currently at fifty percent capacity. Consequently, the
pace of planting for the Rabi 2018/19 season has been lower by ten percent than the normal five-year
average, with significant reductions in acreage of rice and coarse cereals.
