Report Highlights:
Post forecasts Bulgaria’s rapeseed area and yield in marketing year (MY) 2019/20 will be lower due to
drought conditions during the 2018 fall planting season, followed by insufficient 2018/19 winter
precipitation. Many farmers replanted what had been rapeseed fields with spring crops, chiefly
sunflower and corn. Favorable early spring planting conditions and beneficial mid-April/early May
rainfall contributed to this trend. Although the April and May rains gave the rapeseed crop a much-
needed boost, yield losses remain likely for this MY. Sub-surface moisture levels are below last year
and pose a risk for the sunflower crop.
General Information:
Weather Overview
Persistent drought conditions during the fall 2018 rapeseed planting season created unfavorable planting
and germination conditions. Inadequate snowfall over the winter did not provide enough soil moisture,
especially for sub-surface levels. The spring arrived early in March with warm and dry weather.
Although these conditions benefited spring planting, they further contributed toward the water deficit.
