Nigeria’s animal feed sector remains underdeveloped, largely due to high production costs. Seventy
percent of the operational costs of most poultry, aquaculture, and other livestock operations go to feed.
The animal feed sector is estimated at more than $2 billion, continuing to attract significant local and
foreign investment in large-scale feed mill operations.
