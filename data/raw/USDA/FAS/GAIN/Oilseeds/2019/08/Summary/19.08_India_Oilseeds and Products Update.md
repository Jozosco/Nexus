Report Highlights:
Estimated soybean production for marketing year (MY) 2019/20 (Oct.-Sept.) is revised lower to 10.5
million metric tons (MMT) on 11.2 million hectares. The national average yield will settle below last
year’s level due to concerns over prevailing weather conditions. After discounting availability of
soybeans and considering strong domestic demand for animal feed (soymeal), the out-year oilmeal
export forecast is lowered 6.2 percent to 3 MMT. Additionally, after remaining flat for the last two
years, out-year edible oil imports will grow 12 percent to 16.4 MMT.
