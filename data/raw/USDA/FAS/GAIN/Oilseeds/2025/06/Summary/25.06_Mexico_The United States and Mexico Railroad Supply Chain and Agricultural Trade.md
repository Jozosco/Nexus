Report Highlights:
In 2024, Mexico imported more than 35 million metric tons of grains from the United States. The North
American freight rail network is an increasingly important mode of transport for handling the growth in
this trade. Based on USDA’s Federal Grain Inspection Service (FGIS) inspection data, more than 63
percent of grain inspected for export to Mexico is shipped by rail, 35 percent by maritime vessel, and
less than 1 percent by truck.
