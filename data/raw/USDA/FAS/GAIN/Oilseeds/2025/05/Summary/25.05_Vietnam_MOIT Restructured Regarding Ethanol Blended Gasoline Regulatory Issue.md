Report Highlights:
This report provides an update on Vietnam’s government restructuring related to bioethanol-blended
gasoline. As part of recent efforts to streamline government operations, the Ministry of Industry and
Trade did not merge with other ministries but instead reorganized internally. Oversight of ethanol-
blended gasoline regulation, trade, and distribution now falls under a newly created office, the Agency
for Domestic Market Surveillance and Development.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Summary
One of the responsibilities of Vietnam’s Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) is to oversee the
production, trade, and distribution of petroleum products, including gasoline, natural gas, liquefied gas,
and other related products, in accordance with the law. This authority is outlined in Decree 40/2025,
issued by the Government of Vietnam on February 25, 2025.
