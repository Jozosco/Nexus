Report Highlights:
This report provides an update on the restructuring of Vietnam’s government regarding the regulation of
plants and plant products. As part of recent efforts to streamline government operations, the newly
named Plant Production and Protection Department, under the Ministry of Agriculture and Environment,
is now responsible for overseeing plant and plant product regulations. This Department was formed
through the merger of the Department of Crop Production and the Department of Plant Protection.
