Report Highlights:
Marketing Year (MY) 2025/26 European Union oilseed production is forecast to rebound from low
production levels in MY 2024/25 due to better growing conditions; higher, more average yields; and
increases in area harvested. Feed use of oilseed meals is forecast to marginally decline as oilseed
products face price competition from feed grains. MY 2025/26 oilseed imports are anticipated to decline
as better domestic harvests meet more of local demand. Food use of vegetable oils is forecast to rebound
from low-supply conditions in MY 2024/25. The postponed EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)
comes into force at the end of 2025, and will likely impact the overall dynamics of the oilseeds market
particularly for soybean products and palm oil. The Russian invasion of Ukraine continues to bring
uncertainty.
