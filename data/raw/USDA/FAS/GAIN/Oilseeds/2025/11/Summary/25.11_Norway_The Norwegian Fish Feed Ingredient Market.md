Report Highlights:
Norway is the biggest producer of fish in Europe and the number one seafood exporter in the world. In
2024, 2.26 MMT of salmonid fish feed was used with a total estimated value of $3.16 billion for the
ingredients, of which about $2.80 billion were imported. All plant protein ingredients are non-
genetically modified and predominantly imported from Brazil. Most of the fishmeal and fish oil is
sourced from the Nordic European region and South America. A part of the fish oil ($75 million in
2024) is sourced from the United States. Besides a brief overview of the Norwegian fish feed market,
this report includes also the Norwegian import requirements of the most used ingredients.
