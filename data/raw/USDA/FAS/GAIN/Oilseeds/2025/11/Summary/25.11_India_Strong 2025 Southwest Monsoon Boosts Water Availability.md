Report Name:Strong 2025 Southwest Monsoon Boosts Water Availability
Country: India

Report Category: Agricultural Situation, Cotton and Products, Grain and Feed, Oilseeds and Products,
Agriculture in the News
Prepared By: Dhruv Sood
Approved By: Nicole Podesta
Report Highlights:
The 2025 southwest monsoon featured an early onset, overall surplus rainfall, and notable regional
disparities, with deficits in the east and northeast but surpluses in central India. The season ended
slightly late on October 16, with a particularly wet September highlighting increasing rainfall variability.
India received 937.2 millimeters (mm) of rainfall during the southwest monsoon season (June 1 to
September 30), surpassing the normal of 868.6 mm by eight percent. Kharif (monsoon) sowing rose by
one percent, led by gains in rice, maize, and sugarcane, while oilseeds, cotton, and pulses declined.
Reservoir storage remained strong at over 91 percent of capacity, ensuring good water availability for
the upcoming rabi (winter) season.
