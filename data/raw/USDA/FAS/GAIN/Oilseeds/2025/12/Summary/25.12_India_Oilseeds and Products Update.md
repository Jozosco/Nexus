Report Name: Oilseeds and Products Update
Country: India

Report Category: Oilseeds and Products
Prepared By: Shilpita Das, Agricultural Specialist
Approved By: Joanna Brown, Agricultural Attaché
Report Highlights:
India’s peanut oilseed harvested area for marketing year (MY) 2025/26 is projected to reach 5.6 million
hectares, a three percent increase, as farmers shift from cotton to peanuts due to better financial returns
and timely rainfall. Production is forecasted to grow two percent to 7.5 million metric tons (MMT), with
stable yields of 1.33 metric tons per hectare, supported by favorable monsoon conditions. Exports are
expected to rise 16 percent to 928 thousand metric tons (TMT), driven by global trade opportunities and
the India-UK Free Trade Agreement. Ending stocks are anticipated to increase five percent to 500 TMT,
potentially lowering market prices. Peanut oilmeal production and consumption are projected to remain
unchanged at 1.8 MMT and 1.7 MMT, respectively, while peanut oil production is estimated to hold
steady at 1.4 MMT.
