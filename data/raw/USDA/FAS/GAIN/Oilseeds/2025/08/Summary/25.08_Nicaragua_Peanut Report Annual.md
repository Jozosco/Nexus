Report Highlights:
Nicaragua’s peanut farmers are expected to reduce harvested areas by at least five percent in marketing
year (MY) 2025/26 in anticipation of lower prices due to increased Brazilian peanut production. FAS
Managua expects farmers to be more rigorous in selecting production areas based on historical yields in
MY 2025/26, excluding marginal lands with less fertile soil. Even with fluctuating market prices and
adjustments to planted areas, Nicaragua is expected to remain a stable peanut producer in the region,
with exports of shelled peanuts exceeding 70,000 metric tons annually.
