Voluntary Report – Voluntary - Public Distribution Date: August 29, 2025
Report Number: UP2025-0026
Report Name:Oilseeds Update for August 2025
Country: Ukraine
Post: Kyiv
Report Category: Oilseeds and Products
Prepared By: Denys Sobolev
Approved By: Megan Francic
Report Highlights:
Post updated its marketing year (MY) 2025/26 oilseed planted area estimates on official Ukrainian data
and MY2025/26 rapeseed production on actual harvest information. Ukraine is introducing 10 percent
export duties for rapeseed and soybeans, which may affect the crush-to-production ratio, particularly for
soybeans in MY2025/26.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Abbreviations:
CY – Calendar Year
ha – Hectare
MY – Marketing Year
MT – Metric Ton
MMT – Million Metric Ton
NDVI – Normalized Difference Vegetation Index
PSD – Production, Supply, and Distribution
SSSU – State Statistics Service of Ukraine
This report focuses on production and export forecasts for marketing year (MY) 2025/26, which runs
from July 2025 to June 2026 for rapeseed and from October 2025 to September 2026 for sunflower
seeds and soybeans. It also contains updated export estimates for MY2024/25 based on available trade
data. For detailed information on estimates and a general overview of Ukraine's agriculture sector, as
well as palm oil estimates, see our 2025 Oilseeds and Products Annual (UP2025-0008).
