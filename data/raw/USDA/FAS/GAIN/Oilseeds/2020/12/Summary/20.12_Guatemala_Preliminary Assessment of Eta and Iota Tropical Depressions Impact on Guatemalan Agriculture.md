Approved By: Rachel Nelson
Report Highlights:
In November, two category four hurricanes made landfall in Central America. Both reached Guatemala
as tropical depressions and had a severe impact on agricultural production. Total known losses to date
include 165,000 hectares of affected crops valued at $4.3 million. Significant losses have been reported
in the black bean, corn, rice, rubber, palm oil, coffee, and horticultural crops. In the animal sector, the
losses report includes poultry, cattle, swine, horses, small ruminants, and aquaculture fish. The storms
affected close to 286,000 farmers.
