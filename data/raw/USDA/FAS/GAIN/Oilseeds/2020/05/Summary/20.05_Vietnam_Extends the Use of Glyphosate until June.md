Report Highlights:
On April 24, 2020, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD) issued Circular
06/2020 to extend the use of plant protection products containing glyphosate in Vietnam to June 30,
2021. This Circular takes effect on June 9, 2020, one day prior to the effective date of the previous ban
under MARD’s Decision 1186.

AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Summary
On April 24, 2020, MARD issued Circular 06/2020 amending Circular 10/2019 on the List of
Permissible Plant Protection Products and the List of Prohibited Plant Protection Products in Vietnam.
This Circular deactivates the glyphosate ban under MARD’s Decision 1186 dated April 10, 2019 and
extends the use of glyphosate products to June 30, 2021. Vietnam continues to maintain maximum
residue levels (MRLs) for glyphosate in accordance with CODEX MRLs as prescribed in the Ministry
of Health’s Circular 50/2016 (GAIN Report VM7026).
