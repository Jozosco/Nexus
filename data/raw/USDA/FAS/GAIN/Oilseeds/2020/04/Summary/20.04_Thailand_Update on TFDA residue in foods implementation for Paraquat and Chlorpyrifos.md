Country: Thailand
Post: Bangkok
Report Category: Grain and Feed, Trade Policy Monitoring, Oilseeds and Products, Strawberries,
Potatoes and Potato Products, Stone Fruit
Prepared By: Maysa Chanikornpradit, Agricultural Specialist
Approved By: Russell Nicely
Report Highlights:
The Thai Food and Drug Administration (TFDA) has yet to amend its notification to apply a zero
tolerance on the Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) for Paraquat and Chlorpyrifos. Post is monitoring the
development of TFDA’s revised notification on these residues to avoid trade disruption for some U.S.
agricultural commodities and high-value products.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
On April 1, 2020, Thailand notified the WTO of the Ministry of Industry’s (MOI) draft Notification
(G/TBT/N/THA/567) regarding the recategorization of the agricultural chemicals Chlorpyrifos and
Paraquat from a category 3 chemical (subject to restriction) to a category 4 chemical (prohibited for
production, importation, exportation, and possession) with an enforcement date of June 1, 2020. By
mandate of the National Hazardous Substances Committee (NHSC), the Thai Food and Drug
