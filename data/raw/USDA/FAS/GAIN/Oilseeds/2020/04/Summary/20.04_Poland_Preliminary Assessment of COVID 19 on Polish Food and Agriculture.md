Report Highlights:
The Government of Poland (GOP) introduced strict social distancing measures including closure of
borders, limiting commercial air traffic, and restricting cross-border rail travel. Border clearances for
imported food and feed products are functioning without major obstacles. On March 31, 2020 the
GOP announced an economic support program to mitigate the negative affects of the pandemic.
Poland’s hotel, restaurant, and institutional (HRI) sectors were severely affected by COVID-19 and
subsequent business closures. Retail sales for staple products remain robust, while sales of high-value
product sales are lower.
