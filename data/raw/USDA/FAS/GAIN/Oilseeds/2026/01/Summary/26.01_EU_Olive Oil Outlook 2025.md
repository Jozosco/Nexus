Report Name:EU Olive Oil Outlook 2025

Prepared By: Marta Guerrero
Approved By: Evgenia Ustinova
Report Highlights:
The EU is the world’s largest producer of olive oil, accounting for well over half of global supply. In
MY 2025/26, Post projects a continued recovery of the EU olive oil sector from recent droughts, with
production stabilizing, consumption rebounding, and trade flows adapting to both internal and external
market dynamics. The MY 2025/26 olive oil production in the EU is expected to stay marginally below
previous season levels, as long-awaited fall precipitation only arrived in the second half of November,
leaving little room for initial production forecasts to expand. The larger availability of olive oil in MY
2024/25 allowed for larger sales within the EU, and for a recovery in exported volumes, with relatively
smaller import needs.
