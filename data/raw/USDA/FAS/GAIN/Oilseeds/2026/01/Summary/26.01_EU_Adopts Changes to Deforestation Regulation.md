Report Highlights:
On December 23, 2025, the European Union published Regulation 2025/2650, which is an amendment
to the controversial EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR). The published amendments officially delay
the EUDR’s entry into application until December 30, 2026. The amendments also create new
definitions that provide simplifications to some, primarily EU, operators, and excludes paper products
from the scope of application. The new Regulation also calls on the Commission to publish a
simplification review of EUDR by April 30, 2026.

postpones the entry into application of the EUDR by one year, to December 30, 2026, for most
