Products, Oilseeds and Products, Grain and Feed
Prepared By: Dhruv Sood
Approved By: Rhiannon Elms
Report Highlights:
On April 15, the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) forecasted a 31 percent chance of an above
normal 2024 southwest monsoon. IMD will issue a end of May forecast for the Monsoon Core Zone
(MCZ), which represents most rainfed agriculture regions in the country and is the critical forecast
related to agricultural planning, planting, and production. Extreme temperatures were recorded
throughout the country in April, with east India experiencing its hottest April since 1901, while south
India recorded its second hottest April. Above normal high temperatures are forecast from May to June
in most parts of the country.
