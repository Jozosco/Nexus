Report Highlights:
Indonesia’s B40 biodiesel blending mandate program, which is expected to roll out in 2025, is
projected to increase palm oil use by 3 percent to 22 million metric tons in 2024/25. Soybean
consumption recovered in 2023/24 as soybean retail prices continue to decrease. Weak
performance in the feed mill industry reduced 2023/24 soybean meal use lower than previously
expected. Due to lower prices, Indonesia’s imports of U.S. soybean meal rose an estimated 43
percent to 167,000 MT in 2023/24.
