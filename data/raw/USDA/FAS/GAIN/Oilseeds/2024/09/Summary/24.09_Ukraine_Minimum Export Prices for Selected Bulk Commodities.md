Prepared By: Denys Sobolev

Report Highlights:
Ukraine introduced procedures for the approval of minimum export prices for selected bulk
commodities, including grains, oilseeds, vegetable oils and meals, walnuts, and honey.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
On August 21, 2024, The Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine adopted the Resolution #944 “On Approval of
the Procedure for Setting of Minimum Export Prices for Certain Types of Goods.” The Ukrainian
version is available via this link and attached to this report.
According to the resolution, the Ministry of Agrarian Policy and Food of Ukraine (MAPFU) determines
the minimum export prices, taking into account the terms related to the delivery of commodities based
on Incoterms for a limited list of commodities:
 Honey (HS Code 0409 00 00 00)
 Walnuts (HS Codes 0802 31 00 00 and 0802 32 00 00)
 Wheat (HS Code 1001)
 Rye (HS Code 1002)

 Corn (HS Code 1005)
