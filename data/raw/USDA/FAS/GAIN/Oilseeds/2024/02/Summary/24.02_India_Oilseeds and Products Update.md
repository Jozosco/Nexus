Prepared By: Shilpita Das, Agricultural Specialist

Report Highlights:
FAS New Delhi estimates India’s rapeseed-mustard production for marketing year (MY) 2023/2024
(October-September) at 11.9 million metric tons (MMT), harvested from 9.3 million hectares following
favorable cold weather during the rabi crop season. Trade for rapeseed oil remains negligible due to high
domestic demand. Production of rapeseed meal is expected to be around 6.2 MMT owning to a good
oilseed production year. Exports of rapeseed meal will remain relatively high for the current MY due to
increased crushing. India is likely to continue importing a significant amount of discounted edible oils to
meet it domestic demand for vegetable oils.
