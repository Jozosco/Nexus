Summary
Soybeans are a major protein source in food and animal feed in Burma. Burmese food producers
primarily rely on domestically produced soybeans to make local soy food products, such as tofu,
soymilk, soy paste, soy sauce, and snacks. Burmese feed millers tend to rely on imported soybean meal
and domestic soybean cakes for the protein component of animal feed. There are some imports of food-
grade soybeans to produce soymilk.
