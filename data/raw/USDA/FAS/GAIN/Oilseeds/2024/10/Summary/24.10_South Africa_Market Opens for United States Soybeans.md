Report Highlights:
Soybeans from the United States are once again eligible to enter South Africa. After a mid-
summer drought that caused a 35 percent drop in production, South Africa needs to import
soybeans to supplement domestic production and maintain crushing demand. South Africa only
issues import permits for countries that are cultivating genetically engineered (GE) events that
have been approved for food and feed purposes or general release in South Africa. Post worked
closely with stakeholders to resolve the asynchronous issue to allow trade with the United States.
On September 30, 2024, South Africa’s Department of Agriculture informed stakeholders that all
GE soybean events that caused asynchrony with the United States had been approved and that
import permits will be issued for GE soybeans from the United States.
