Below-normal rains, high temperatures, and less water availability from major reservoirs reportedly
slowed kharif (fall and early harvested) crop planting progress; this is expected to affect negatively both
rainfed and irrigated crop production. MY 2014/15 total edible oil imports are estimated at 12.3 MMT,
up 12 percent on competitive international prices, growing demand from middle class consumers, and
lower domestic oilseed supplies.
