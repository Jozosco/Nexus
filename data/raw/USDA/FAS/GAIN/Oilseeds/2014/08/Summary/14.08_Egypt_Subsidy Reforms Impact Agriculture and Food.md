Report Highlights:
This report details the fuel and food subsidy reforms in Egypt. Changes in the food subsidy system,
both in regards to the ration card and bread subsidy, will impact both consumption and trade
patterns.
General Information:
Energy and food subsidies have been a significant part of the Egyptian government’s budget,
constituting around 7% of total GDP. In 2013/14, energy subsidies amounted to 143 billion LE,
while food subsidies accounted for 31 billion LE of the government budget (Note: the exchange rate
on August 10, 2014 is 7.15 Egyptian pounds [LE] to $1).
In July 2014, the government of Egypt announced major changes to the subsidy program. Regarding
energy subsidies, prices for three widely-used subsidized fuels were raised by up to 78 percent, while
diesel prices increased by 64 percent. The government outlined a plan to raise electricity prices over
the next five years.
