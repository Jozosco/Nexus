Report Highlights:
Domestically grown protein crops were the focus of a recent symposium hosted by the German
Government. The symposium is part of the national ‘Protein Strategy,’ which aims to reduce
Germany’s dependence on soybean and soymeal imports. The Government will spend 15 million Euros
until 2017 for research and demonstration networks.
