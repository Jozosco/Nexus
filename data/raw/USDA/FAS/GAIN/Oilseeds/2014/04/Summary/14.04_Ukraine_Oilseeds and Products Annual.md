Overall oilseed area in Ukraine in Marketing Year (MY) 2014/15 is projected to increase due to expansion of
soybean plantings (by 17 percent or up to 1.6 Million Hectares (mln ha) compared to 1.36 mln ha in MY 2013/14).
This crop has shown good results in terms of production and profitability last year and is once again attracting
Ukrainian producers on the expectation of strong world market prices.
