Report Highlights:
Greece is the world’s third largest olive oil producer after Spain and Italy. According to industry
contacts, Greece’s MY2013/14 olive oil production is projected to decline by 57 percent compared to
the previous campaign, because of the severe drought of the summer. More than 80 percent of the
Greek annual production is extra virgin olive oil. Per capita consumption of olive oil in Greece (20
Kg/year) is one of the highest in the world. Ninety percent of Greek olive oil is exported to the
European Union: 80 percent in bulk and 10 percent under Greek brand names.
