Report Highlights:
After plummeting in MY2012/13, when prolonged dry weather halved yields, Spain’s olive oil
production is anticipated to recover to average levels in MY2013/14. The higher supply will allow for a
recovery of exports and stocks. No major changes are anticipated in domestic consumption.
