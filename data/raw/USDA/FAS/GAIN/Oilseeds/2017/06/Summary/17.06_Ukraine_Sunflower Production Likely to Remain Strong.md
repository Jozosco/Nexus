Production:
For general information about oilseed production and trade in Ukraine please refer to our previous
annual GAIN Report UP1706.
According to reports by the Ministry of Agricultural Policy and Food of Ukraine (MAPFU) farmers
have already planted over 5.5 million hectares (ha) of sunflower seed and almost 1.9 million ha of
soybeans as of June 7, 2017. According to the latest MAPFU report, farmers planted 858 thousand ha of
winter rapeseed.
These numbers were used by FAS Kyiv to adjust production volumes of these crops for marketing year
(MY) 2017/18.
