Report Highlights:
Post forecasts 2017/18 production at 107 million metric tons (mmt), a 6.1 percent reduction compared
to the previous season. The drop in the production outlook is due to a return to average yields, after an
almost perfect season in 2016/17. Soybean exports for Marketing Year (MY) 2017/18 are forecast at 64
mmt as a result of strong demand by China. For the current MY 2016/17, Brazil exported 56 mmt in
the first 7 months of the MY, about 19 percent higher compared to the previous season. Post forecasts
exports to reach 65 mmt, a record.
