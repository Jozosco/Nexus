notification to amend the Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales)
Regulations, 2011, and invited comments from the WTO member countries. The amendments relate to
the removal of ‘Boudouin test’ requirement for blended edible vegetable oil and revision of provisions
on the sale of Vanaspati oil. The comment period for trading partners expires 60 days from the date it is
notified on WTO website (www.wto.org).
