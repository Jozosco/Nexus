Report Highlights:
This report provides an unofficial translation of Decision 5463/QD-BNN-PC, dated December 28, 2016
of Vietnam’s Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD). The Decision revokes Circular
20/2014/TT-NNNPTNT, dated June 26, 2014, which specified the roles and responsibilities in the
management of agriculture materials. This Decision took effect from December 28, 2016.
1

veterinary drugs, fertilizers, and animal feeds resides in a number of specialized management agencies
under MARD, including the Department of Plant Protection, Department of Animal Health, Department
of Crop Production, Directorate of Fisheries, and the Department of Livestock Production. However,
since August 2014, MARD’s Circular 20/2014 had protracted the registration process, adding additional
