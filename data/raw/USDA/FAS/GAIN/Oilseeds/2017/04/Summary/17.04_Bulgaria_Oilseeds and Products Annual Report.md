Report Highlights:
Bulgarian farmers are expanding the planted areas under oilseed crops in the new season MY2017/18
led by sunflower seeds. Following mixed results from oilseeds in MY2016/17 with excellent rapeseeds
production, a lower than expected sunflower crop, and disappointing soybean crop, most producers
expect a recovery in sunflower and soybean yields and a decline from the record high rapeseeds yields
this year. Total oilseed area is forecast to increase by 2% - 3% and provided that the weather
cooperates, to lead to 4% - 5% or higher growth in production to 2.4 MMT.
There have been new dynamics on the local market in MY2016/17. Traditionally high sunflower seeds
exports slowed down in the first half of the season at the expense of local processing for crush and for
de-hulling while imports skyrocketed. The rapeseed crop was quickly and almost fully exported soon
after harvest, at the same time the demand for crush later in the season stimulated higher imports.
