Report Highlights:
Taiwan is a mature and stable market. The primary driver behind soybean imports is demand for
soybean meal for animal feed. An outbreak of Avian Influenza (AI) in February 2017 is forecasted to
cause soybean imports to drop slightly in MY2016/17 before recovering in MY2017/18.
Executive Summary:
Taiwan is a mature market with relatively stable demand. The primary driver behind soybean imports is
demand for soybean meal for animal feed. Oil production from domestic crushing is sufficient to meet
domestic oil demand. The little soybeans grown domestically are used for food products on the local
market.
Soybean consumption is forecast to recover slightly in MY2017/18 to 2.41 million tons. MY2016/17
soybean consumption is forecast to drop to 2.38 million tons based on import and animal production
trends. MY2015/16 soybean consumption is lowered to 2.41 million tons based on official soybean
meal production statistics. Almost all soybean demand is supplied by imports, and MY2017/18 and
