Report Highlights:
China continues to be the largest oilseed importer in the world. In MY15/16, China’s total oilseed imports
reached 87.93 million tons (MMT). Chinese total soybean imports hit another record at 83.23 MMT, absorbing
61 percent of total world exports, and 59 percent of total U.S. soybean exports. Post estimates this growing trend
in soybean imports will continue and reach 86 MMT in MY16/17, and 89 MMT in MY17/18. Favorable import
prices led to record peanuts imports in MY15/16 but are expected to level off. Rising incomes, urbanization and
the modernization of the domestic feed and livestock sectors will continue fostering Chinese oilseed consumption.
