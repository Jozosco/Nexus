Report Highlights:
Romania’s oilseed crop in marketing year (MY) 2017/18 will increase by 21 percent over last year,
boosted by bumper sunflower production, as well as strong rapeseed and soybean crops. The increase
follows higher yields due to favorable weather and improved inputs, as well as area expansion. Post
forecasts MY 2017/18 sunflower exports to grow by 30 percent.
General Information:
Total oilseed area and production
Sunflower is Romania’s most important oilseed crop, followed by rapeseed and soy. Romania is the
EU’s leading sunflower producer. MY 2017/18 total oilseed area planted reached nearly 2 million
hectares (HA), a 15-percent increase over last year. Post expects total oilseed output at almost 5.0
million metric tons (MMT), up by 21-percent over last season.
