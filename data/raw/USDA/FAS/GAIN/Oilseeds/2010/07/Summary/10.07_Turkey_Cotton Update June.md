Approved By:

Prepared By:
Ibrahim Sirtioglu
Report Highlights:
MY 2010 Turkish cotton area and production in are now estimated to be 330,000 hectares and 500,000 MT. An increase in
planting is reported in all regions due to high world prices and increased domestic demand. Domestic consumption for MY
2009 is now estimated about 1.2 MMT, about ten percent higher than earlier estimates. MY 2009 imports reached 681,000
MT in the first nine months of the marketing year and year end imports are expected to reach 820,000 MT. High U.S. cotton
prices prevented a similar increase in Turkish imports from the United States, which remained at 312,000 MT (1.4 million
bales) during the first nine months of the year. The GSM-102 Credit Guarantee Program continues to play an important role
for U.S. exports to Turkey.
Production:
MY 2010 cotton area and production are now estimated to be about 330,000 hectares and 500,000 MT (2.3 million bails)
due to larger than anticipated planting as a result of high world cotton prices. Planting increases are reported in all regions
and favorable weather conditions are expected to increase yields.
