Report Highlights:
Indonesia has initiated plans to achieve a goal of self-sufficiency in beef by 2014 through
implementation of an integrated agricultural and livestock production project.
General Information:
The Indonesian government is planning to implement several measures in order to enhance its food
security. Indonesia remains focused on becoming self-sufficient in rice, corn, sugar, soybeans, and
meat. During the “Agriculture and Livestock Integration Towards Food Reliance” national seminar
held at Sultan Syarif Kasim State Islamic University in Pekanbaru, Riau on Monday August 2, 2010, the
Indonesian Minister of Agriculture urged the Riau provincial administration to set aside a minimum of
one million hectares to support an integrated cattle breeding and oil palm program. The minister
estimated that the one million hectares of palm oil could be used to feed two million heads of cattle per
annum. This program is expected to help meet the Indonesian national government’s goal of achieving
self sufficiency in meat by 2014.
