Post has revised upward its forecast of 2010/11 soybean planted area to a record 24 million hectares.

represent a 2.6 percent increase in area planted and a 1.7 percent decrease in production compared to
2009/10. The La Nina weather phenomenon slightly delayed planting and is expected to reduce
national average yields due to irregular precipitation patterns. Favorable futures prices have spurred
early committed sales of the 2010/11 soybean crop to 18 percent, ahead of historical norms.
