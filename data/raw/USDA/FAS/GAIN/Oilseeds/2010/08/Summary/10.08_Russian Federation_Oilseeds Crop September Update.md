Report Highlights:
Area sown to oilseeds in Russia increased in MY 2010 by 20 percent to 9.7 million hectares. However,
drought in the European Russia seriously affected yields of sunflowerseeds, and production might not
increase from last year’s 6.4 million metric tons. Rainy weather in the Far East may decrease soybean
yields compared with last year, and production may increase only by 6 percent to 1.0 MMT.
General Information:
Oilseeds sown area

2010 by 20 percent to almost 9.7 million hectares. Sunflowerseeds, soybeans, and rapeseed, Russia’s
three major oilseed crops, were sown on 9.3 million hectares: 7.2 million hectares of sunflowerseeds (16
percent increase from last year), 1.2 million hectares of soybeans (37 percent increase from last year),
