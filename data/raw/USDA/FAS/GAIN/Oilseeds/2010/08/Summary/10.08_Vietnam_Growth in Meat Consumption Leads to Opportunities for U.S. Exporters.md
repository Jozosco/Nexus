Report Highlights:
1
With Vietnam’s incredible poverty reduction over the past ten years and its continuously increasing
Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Vietnamese citizens are able to afford more protein in their
diets. Many Vietnamese fulfill this dietary requirement with pork supplied by domestic and foreign
producers. With the increased demand for pork, Vietnamese producers need to become more efficient
in order to be able to supply local demand, while also being able to compete with foreign
imports. Currently, U.S. export opportunities to Vietnam for pork to meet local demand and for feed
grains and soybean meal to supply the country’s growing livestock industry have never been more
promising.
