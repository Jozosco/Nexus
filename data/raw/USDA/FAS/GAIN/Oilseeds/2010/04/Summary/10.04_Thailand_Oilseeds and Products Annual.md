A completion of two new soybean oil crushing plants will increase the demand for imported soybeans
in MY 2010/11. This expansion will help the two largest crushers in Thailand lower their production
costs and improve their competitive position in the domestic soybean meal and international soybean
oil markets.
In MY 2008/09 the U.S. market share of Thai soybean imports dropped to its lowest level in recent
history at 14 percent, however in MY2009/10 the market share is estimated to double to 28 percent
due to tighter supplies from Brazil and Argentina driven by dry conditions. In MY 2010/11 it is
expected that the U.S. market will drop again as ample crops from these countries will replenish
dwindling stocks.
