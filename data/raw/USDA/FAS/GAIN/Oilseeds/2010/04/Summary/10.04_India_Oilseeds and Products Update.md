Report Highlights:
According to the Indian Ministry of Agriculture’s progressive planting report, area planted to rapeseed, peanut, and
sunflower decreased 4 percent to 8.2 million hectares, with the sunflower and rapeseed/mustard crop accounting for most
of the decline. The edible oil import forecast for 2009/10 is revised upwards to 8.6 million tons. During first five
months of 2009/10, the total sales commitment of U.S. origin soy oil to India was 108,300 tons which is almost 25
percent of total Indian soy oil imported to date. The oil meal export forecast for 2009/10 (which includes mostly
soybean and rapeseed meal) is revised down by 1.1 million tons to 3.1 million tons due to weak export demand.
