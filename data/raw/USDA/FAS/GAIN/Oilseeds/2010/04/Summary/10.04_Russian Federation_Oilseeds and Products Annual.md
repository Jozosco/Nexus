Report Highlights:
Russia’s production of the main oilseeds crops (sunflowerseeeds, soybeans and rapeseeds) in 2010 is
forecast at 8.5 million metric tons (MMT), including 6.8 MMT of sunflowerseeds. Demand for protein
feeds will be growing and production of oilseeds meal is forecast at 4.3 MMT. Production of vegetable
oil is forecast at 3.2 MMT. In 2010 Russia will continue to increase imports of soybeans. Imports of
soybean meal are forecast to increase to 0.5 MMT in spite of a 5 percent import duty. These imports
will be driven by high domestic demand for protein feeds. Exports of meal and oil are forecast at 1.0
MMT and 0.9 MMT respectively; slightly lower than in MY 2009. The decrease was caused by high
domestic demand and strengthening ruble.
