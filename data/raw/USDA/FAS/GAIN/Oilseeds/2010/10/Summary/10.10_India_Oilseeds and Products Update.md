Report Highlights:
Total area planted to kharif oilseeds, which includes mostly peanuts, soybean and sunflower, was 14.5 million hectares.
Good monsoon rains in ‘kharif 2010’ facilitated oilseed planting progress; with peanut crop accounting for most of the
increase in acreage. The edible oil import forecast for MY 2010/11 is revised up marginally to 8.7 million tons. The import
basket includes 6.8 million tons of palm oil, 1.4 million tons of soybean oil, and 500,000 tons of sunflower seed oil and
45,000 tons of other edible oils. Importer interest is expected to continue, especially in light of the zero import duty on
crude edible oils. Oil meal export forecast for MY 2010/11 is revised up to 4.9 million tons.
