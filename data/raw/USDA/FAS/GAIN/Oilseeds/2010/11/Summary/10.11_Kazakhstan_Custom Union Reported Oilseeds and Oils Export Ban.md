Report Highlights:
Although Kazakhstan was not as severely affected by drought and bad weather as the Russian
Federation, it banned for 6 months exports of buckwheat, major oilseeds, and vegetable and animal oils
and fats. The Custom Union Commission invited Russia and Belarus to join the ban.
General Information:
Kazak Agriculture did not suffer as severely as Russia from the 2010 drought and fires; however, the
Government decided to introduce a 6-months’ ban on exports of buckwheat, oilseeds, and vegetable
oils and animal fats in order to maintain domestic supply with these commodities. In 2009
Kazakhstan was an insignificant exporter of these commodities. Surprisingly the ban includes all
countries including other Custom Union members. The list of commodities with the Custom Codes
of the Foreign Trade Activities of the Customs Union (TN VED TC) is the following:
- Buckwheat (code 1008 10 000);
- Buckwheat groats (Code 1103 19 900 9);
- Soybeans (Code 1201 00);
- Sunflowerseeds (Code 1206 00);
- Cottonseeds (Code 1207 20);
- Other oilseeds (Code 1207 9
