Report Highlights:
Extremely wet weather and floods in the Czech Republic will decrease this year’s harvest by 10-
15%, according to the Agrarian Chamber and the Ministry of Agriculture. Worst hit were spring
crops, like malting barley that will have significantly lower yields. Nevertheless, the exportable
surplus of grains is expected to be available.
General Information:
According to the latest estimates of the Agrarian Chamber, 34,822 hectares of agricultural land were
damaged by the recent extreme precipitation and subsequent floods. The South-Moravian, Olomouc,
Zlin and Moravian-Silesian regions were affected most. The damaged area accounts for just 3% of
the total agricultural land in the Czech Republic. The most damaged crops are grains, rapeseed and
vegetables.
Total harvest of grains is estimated 10-15% lower compared to last year. Last year’s harvest was very
good, above average, reaching over 7.8 million MT. This year, there is still expected to be an
exportable surplus between 1.5 – 1.7 million MT.
Ninety percent of winter crops survived. Spring crops suffered from the cold and rain
