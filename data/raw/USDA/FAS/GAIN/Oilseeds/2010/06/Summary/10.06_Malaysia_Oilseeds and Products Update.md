Report Highlights:
Malaysia's crude palm oil (CPO) production dropped 5.8 percent to 1.3 MMT in April 2009. Post revised CPO output
slightly upwards to 18.5 MMT for 2009/10. PO exports declined 8.2 percent to 1.3 MMT in April. Lower CPO output
resulted in a lower stock level at the end of April. In line with a softer global oilseed market, the monthly average
delivered CPO price declined to RM2,535/MT in May.
Post:
Kuala Lumpur
Executive Summary:
Production
The Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) reported that total crude palm oil (CPO) production dropped 5.8 percent to 1.3
MMT in April 2010. Peninsular Malaysia’s production dropped 7.4 percent while East Malaysia’s output was also down
by 3.8 percent. Compared to April 2009, output during April 2010 was 26 percent higher. Despite a dip in April, the
industry is expecting a seasonal uptrend in output till October. Post expects CPO output to be about 4.4 TMT during the
Apr-Jun quarter.
