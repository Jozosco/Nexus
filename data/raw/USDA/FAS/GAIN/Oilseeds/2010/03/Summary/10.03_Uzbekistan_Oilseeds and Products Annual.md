Report Highlights:
Uzbekistan’s MY2009/10 total oilseeds production is forecast to decrease to 1.85 MMT and MY10/11
is preliminarily forecast at 2.1 MMT. Oilseeds imports will increase to 80,000 tons in MY2010/11,
including 50,000 tons of soybeans from Brazil. MY09/10 total oil meal imports are estimated to
increase to 20,000 tons and MY10/11 accordingly to 30,000 tons. Vegetable oil imports are forecast to
increase to 70,000 MT in MY2009/10
