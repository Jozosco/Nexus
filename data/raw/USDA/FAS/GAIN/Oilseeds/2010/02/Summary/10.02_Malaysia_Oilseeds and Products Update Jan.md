Executive Summary
Production
The Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) reported that total crude palm oil (CPO) production dropped 4.7 percent to 1.5
MMT in December 2009, bringing the total CPO output to 17.6 MMT for CY2009. For December, Peninsular
Malaysia’s production dropped 6.9 percent while East Malaysia’s output was also down by 2.2 percent. Compared to
December 2008, output during December 2009 was 2.5 percent higher. The palms are experiencing a seasonal
downtrend in output with a turnaround expected in March. Post now expects CPO output to be about 3.8 TMT during
Jan-Mar quarter.
