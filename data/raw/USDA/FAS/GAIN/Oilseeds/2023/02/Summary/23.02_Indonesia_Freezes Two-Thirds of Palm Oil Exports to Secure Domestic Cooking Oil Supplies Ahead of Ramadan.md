Report Highlights:
On February 6, the Government of Indonesia (GOI) announced it would suspend two-thirds of
palm oil exports in order to shore up domestic cooking oil supplies ahead of Ramadan and Eid
festivities. The GOI has set a target of increasing domestic cooking oil supplies by 50 percent. A
projected 400,000 to 800,000 metric tons (MT) of Indonesian palm oil are expected to be
blocked from the global market over the next three months.
