Rice Prices Decline
After holding steady for the past few months, rice prices declined slightly during the week of January
29, 2023. The coarse rice price dropped to BDT 50 ($0.48) per kilogram, down about 4 percent from the
previous week, as the newly harvested Aman rice increased supply in the local market (Figure 1).
The price of medium quality rice also dropped 4 percent from the previous week to BDT 58 ($0.55) per
kilogram, while the price of high-quality rice remained unchanged at BDT 75 ($0.71) per kilogram.
