Report Highlights:
This report provides an overview of recent trends in Japan’s expanding market for plant-based meat
alternatives. These products are typically derived from ingredients such as soybeans, wheat, peas and
broad beans. In recent years, an increasing number of Japanese food manufacturing and startup
companies have launched new plant-based products to provide an alternative to animal-based protein
sources.
