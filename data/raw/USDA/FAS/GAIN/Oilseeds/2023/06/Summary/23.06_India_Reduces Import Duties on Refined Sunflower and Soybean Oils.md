Report Highlights:
On June 14, 2023, India’s Ministry of Finance issued Public Notice No. 39/2023 - Customs that reduces
the import duty on refined sunflower and soybean oils. As of June 15, 2023, the basic customs duty
(BCD) for both refined oils are lowered from 17.5 percent to 12.5 percent, which aligns with the
government’s import duty for refined, bleached deodorized (RBD) palm olein. The move is intended to
control fluctuating edible oil prices.
