Rice Prices Remain High, but Steady
This week, the coarse rice price remained unchanged from last week at BDT 52 ($0.50) per kilogram
(Figure 1). As mentioned in last week's report, the current price of all types of rice is about 7 percent
higher than the same period last year. Usually, rice prices decline at harvest time; however, high
production costs are leading to high paddy prices and high rice prices this year.
This week, the price of medium quality rice increased slightly to BDT 60 ($0.57) per kilogram, up about
3 percent from the previous week, while the price of fine quality rice remained steady at BDT 75 ($0.71)
per kilogram.
