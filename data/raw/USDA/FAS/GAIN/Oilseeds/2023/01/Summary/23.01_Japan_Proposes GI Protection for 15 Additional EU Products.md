General Information
On January 12, 2023, Japan’s Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) published for public
comment a list of 15 newly proposed geographical indications (GIs) for food products from the European Union
(EU). The products include 7 types of cheese, 2 types of sausages, 2 types of olive oil, vinegar, peach, lemon and
garlic. Please see JA2021-0112 and JA2020-0141 for information about MAFF’s earlier GI proposals for EU
products.
