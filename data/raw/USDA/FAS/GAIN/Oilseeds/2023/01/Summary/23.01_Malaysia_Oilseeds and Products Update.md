Report Highlights:

THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF AND NOT NECESSARILY
STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Summary:
Post maintains marketing year (MY) 2022/23 palm oil production at 19 million metric tons (MT).
Although a harsher-than-normal tropical monsoon season will likely have a negative effect on
production in the first quarter of 2023, the losses are expected to be compensated by high production in
mid-2023 onwards. The labor situation slowly improves with more foreign workers expected to arrive in
2023 contributing to improved production for MY 2022/23.
Exports for MY 2022/23 are maintained at 16.45 million MT, the small increase in exports is attributed
to China’s relaxed Zero-COVID policy and India’s steady import tax policy on palm oil. India and
China remain the largest export markets for Malaysian palm oil.
