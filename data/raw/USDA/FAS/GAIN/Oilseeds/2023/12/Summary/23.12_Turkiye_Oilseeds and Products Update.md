Report Highlights:
The olive harvest continues in Turkiye. The Marketing Year (MY) 2023/24 olive oil production forecast is revised
to 190,000 MT due to unfavorable climatic conditions. Domestic olive oil prices in Turkey have more than tripled
(in Turkish Lira) compared to last year. According to an October 18, 2023, press release issued by the Ministry of
Trade regarding the implementation of the measure on the export of olive oil in bulk and in barrels, the
restrictions announced on August 1 and scheduled to last until November 1, 2023 have been extended until
further notice.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Production – Olive Oil
The MY 2023/24 olive oil production forecast is revised downward from the USDA official projection
to 190,000 MT. According to industry sources, this year's harvest, which started in October, is less than
half of last year's production due to being an off year and weather. Specifically, unfavorable climatic
conditions during the flowering period caused total production to decrease significantly compared to the
previous year.
