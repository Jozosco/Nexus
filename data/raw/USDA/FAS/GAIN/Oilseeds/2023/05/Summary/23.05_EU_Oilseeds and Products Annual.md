MY 2023/24 European Union oilseed production is forecast to increase by over four percent over the

higher average yields, and increased area. Feed use of oilseed meals is forecast down, in line with
declining livestock and dairy sectors. Biofuels and food use of vegetable oils are forecast to remain
stable. The Russian invasion of Ukraine still impacts the EU and world oilseeds market.
