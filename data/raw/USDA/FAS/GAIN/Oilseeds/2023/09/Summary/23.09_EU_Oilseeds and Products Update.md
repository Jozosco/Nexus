Report Highlights:
This report updates several sections of the European Union’s “Oilseeds and Products Annual” report

about 2 percent and increased average yields are forecast to result in a 6 percent higher production of
total three major oilseeds in MY 2023/24. Compared to the previous MY, where heat and drought
significantly reduced yields of especially late crops, soybean production is forecast to be over 16 percent
up, sunflower production 12 percent up, and rapeseed production almost 2 percent up.
