Report Highlights:
On June 11, 2020, the Government of Vietnam (GVN) officially announced in Resolution 91 the
suspension of the Certificate Free Sale (CFS) requirement for imported feed and feed ingredients for
further review and amendment, as part of the Government’s Regular Meeting in May. This report is an
addendum to GAIN Report VM2020-0017 dated March 20, 2020, in which the Ministry of Agriculture
and Rural Development (MARD) initially announced the suspended implementation of the CFS
requirement.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Summary

proposal to the GVN for an official suspension. After reviewing MARD’s proposal to suspend the CFS
requirement due to problems and shortcomings in the implementation process, the GVN officially
