Country: Ukraine

Prepared By: Denys Sobolev

Report Highlights:
Chinese authorities approved four rapeseed meal processing facilities for export to China.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
According to information received from the State Service for Food Safety and Consumer Protection (in
Ukrainian) Chinese authorities approved four Ukrainian rapeseed meal processing facilities for export to
China. The list of approved facilities is available here.
In completing this step China and Ukraine are progressing in their mutually agreed upon Protocol for
veterinary and phytosanitary requirements for exports of rapeseed meal to China (Protocol) signed on
November 27, 2019. Please refer to our GAIN Report UP2019-0051 for more details.
Rapeseed production is gaining popularity among Ukrainian farmers. The potential of exporting
rapeseed meal to China may further boost Ukrainian rapeseed processing volumes as Ukraine has an
excess oilseed crushing capacity. Please refer to our GAIN Report UP2020-0020 for more information
about the Ukrainian oilseed market.
