Report Highlights:
Thailand’s National Hazardous Substance Committee (NHSC) convened on April 30, a month before
the WTO comment submission deadline, to reaffirm its previous decision to ban Paraquat and
Chlorpyrifos effective June 1, 2020. Post anticipates that a zero-tolerance for residues of these two
active ingredients (AIs) in food ingredients and food products will be endorsed by May 30, 2020, which
will affect the shipments of U.S. bulk commodities particularly soybeans and wheat hereafter.
