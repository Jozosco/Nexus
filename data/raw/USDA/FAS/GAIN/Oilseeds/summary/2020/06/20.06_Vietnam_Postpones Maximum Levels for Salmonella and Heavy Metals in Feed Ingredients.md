Report Highlights:
On June 30, 2020, the Vietnam Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD) issued
Circular 08/2020 to postpone the implementation of new maximum levels (MLs) for Salmonella and
heavy metals in feed ingredients of plant origin for one year, until July 1, 2021, for further review and
assessment. Vietnam also notified National Technical Regulation (NTR) 190 to the World Trade
Organization (WTO) as G/SPS/N/VNM/113 on June 23, 2020. The comment period for this notification
closes on July 31, 2020.
