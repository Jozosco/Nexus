Report Highlights:
Romania’s grain production is forecast down by 2.6 percent in marketing year (MY) 2020/21 from MY
2019/20. Dry weather conditions will reduce current MY winter crop yields. Soil moisture levels
remain low in eastern and southeastern regions, but are more favorable in western and southwestern
regions. Rains in early May helped winter crops partially recover and provided a strong boost to
recently planted spring crops. Corn exports are expected to reach record levels in MY 2019/20.
