Report Highlights:
This report provides an unofficial translation of the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development
Circular 15/2020/TT-BNNPTNT dated December 25, 2020, promulgating the National Technical
Regulations (NTR) on the phytosanitary requirements for imported regulated articles. The objective of
the NTR is to regulate the quarantine requirements of imported plants. The NTR sets a zero tolerance on
all quarantine pests regulated under Circular 35/2014/TT-BNNPTNT issued in 2014.
