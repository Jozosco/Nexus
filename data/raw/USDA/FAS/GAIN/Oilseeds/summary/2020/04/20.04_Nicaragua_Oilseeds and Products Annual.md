Report Highlights:
Nicaragua peanut production fell 11 percent in marketing year (MY) 2019/2020 dropping to 158,000
metric tons (MT). The decrease was due to limited access to finance caused by Nicaragua’s social
political crisis as well as a 15 percent increase in the cost of agricultural inputs. Post expects production
to rebound slightly in MY 2020/2021 to an estimated 168,000 MT as producers return to planting
historical areas.
