Executive Summary
The outbreak of COVID-19 led to a decline in domestic demand for several food products such as
wheat-based products, chicken meat, pork, ready-to-drink milk, fishery products, and fruits due to
reduced tourism and temporary lockdown measures. The reduced demand has caused some supply
chain disruptions such as an over production of milk and fruit. Demand for staple items, such as eggs
and instant noodles, surged out of a fear of supply shortages. Prices for eggs climbed so quickly that the
Thai government temporarily banned their export
