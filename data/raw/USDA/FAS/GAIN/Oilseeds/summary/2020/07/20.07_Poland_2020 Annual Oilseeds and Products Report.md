Report Highlights:
Post estimates that in marketing year (MY) 2020/2021 (July-June), total Polish rapeseed production will
reach 2.75 million metric tons (MMT), a 17-percent increase over MY 2019/20. The production
increase stems from favorable weather conditions during the fall 2019 planting season, mild winter
weather conditions, early vegetation in spring 2020, and adequate precipitation from May to June 2020.
Post estimates that the MY 2020/2021 area planted for rapeseed is upwards of 800,000 hectares (HA), a
seven-percent decrease from the previous MY. Yields will increase over MY 2019/20 due to earlier
than usual nitrogen application and Poland’s temporary exemption for neonicotinoids plant protection
products for rapeseed production granted by the European Commission (EC).
