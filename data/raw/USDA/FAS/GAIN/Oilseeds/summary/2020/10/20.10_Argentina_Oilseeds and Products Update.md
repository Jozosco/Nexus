Report Highlights:
Despite rising international prices for soybeans in recent months, policy and economic uncertainty is
leading Argentine farmers to hold stored soybeans, resulting in record high projected ending stocks for
the 2019/20 and 2020/21 local crop years. On October 1, Argentina announced temporary reductions in
soybean export taxes and a new 2% differential between export taxes on whole soybeans and underlying
products. Dry weather has prevented farmers from planting sunflowerseed in the far north of Argentina
and will reduce total planted area to 1.685 million hectares. Despite early season dryness, 2020/21
soybean productions is projected up 2.6%, sunflowerseed up 3% and peanut flat compared to 2019/20.
