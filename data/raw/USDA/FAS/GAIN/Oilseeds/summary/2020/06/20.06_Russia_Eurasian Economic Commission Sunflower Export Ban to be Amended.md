EEC authorities plan to extend the current restriction on exports of sunflower seeds outside EAEU
countries through August 31, 2020 in order to “stabilize prices for sunflower oilseeds on the internal
market”. However, the ban will be lifted in favor of a less restrictive regime that only applies to
sunflower seeds.
