Weakening domestic and international demand as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic has significantly
impacted palm oil consumption and exports. Increasing stocks are expected to impact producer margins,
leading to lower production. Soybean imports are lowered based on decreasing demand and rising
prices due to the fluctuating rupiah.
