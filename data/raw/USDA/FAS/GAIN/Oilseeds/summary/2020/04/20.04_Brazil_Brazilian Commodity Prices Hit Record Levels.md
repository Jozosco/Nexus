Report Highlights:
Prices for major Brazilian row crops, including soybeans, corn, rice, and wheat, all hit record highs in
nominal value in recent weeks. The main reason for the domestic price increase is a steep devaluation
of the Brazilian real (BRL). While the BRL has been steadily weakening over the past few years,
economic turmoil driven by the coronavirus pandemic has worsened the trend in 2020. As a result,
despite the deteriorating economic outlook on the whole, Brazilian grain and oilseed producers are
having arguably their most profitable season to date.
