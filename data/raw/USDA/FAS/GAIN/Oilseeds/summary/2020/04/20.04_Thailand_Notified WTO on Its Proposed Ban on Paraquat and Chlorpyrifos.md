Report Highlights:
Thailand notified WTO of the Ministry of Industry’s intention to ban Paraquat and Chlorpyrifos for
production, importation, exportation, and possession. Since these two substances are still used in the
United States, the ban, if enforced, will affect the exports of agricultural commodities, especially
soybeans and wheat, into Thailand.
