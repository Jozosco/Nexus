Report Highlights:
Turkey announced additional tariff reductions on lentils and eliminated tariffs on sunflower seed.
According to a Presidential Decree published in the Official Gazette on November 5, 2020, import
tariffs on red lentils are reduced to 9 percent, down from 19.3 percent, until further notice. Import tariffs
on sunflower seed are reduced to zero through June 2021.
