The Thai Food and Drug Administration (TFDA) extended the public comment period on the Ministry
of Public Health (MOPH)’s draft Notification Re: Food Containing Pesticide Residues (Pesticide
Residues in Food) (No. 3) from June 15, 2020 to July 18, 2020. According to sources, international
entities can submit comments to the TFDA.
