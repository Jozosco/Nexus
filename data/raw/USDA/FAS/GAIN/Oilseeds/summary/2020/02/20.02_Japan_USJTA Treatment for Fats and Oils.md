Report Highlights:
This is one in a series of reports providing concise overviews of how the U.S.-Japan Trade Agreement
(USJTA), which entered into force on January 1, 2020, affects certain product groups. Once USJTA is
fully implemented, nearly 90 percent of all U.S. food and agricultural products imported into Japan will
be duty free or receive preferential tariff access. Please note that the information provided is not fully
comprehensive and additional details may be found in the agreement text itself. Additional
information is available at www.usdajapan.org/usjta.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
