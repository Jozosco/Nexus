Report Highlights:
Bulgaria’s hotel, restaurant, and institutional (HRI) sectors were severely affected by COVID-19 and
subsequent business closures. Border clearances for imported food and feed products are functioning
normally. To date, grain and oilseeds farmers remain fully engaged and viable. Retail sales for staple
products remain robust, while sales of high-value products are lower. Food retail prices are relatively
stable, but hinge on smooth supply chain operations. Unemployment rates jumped sharply due to
COVID-19, with some estimates predicting that as many as 1 million people will be out of work
(Bulgaria’s total population is about 7 million).
