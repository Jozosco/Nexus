Report Highlights:
This report provides an unofficial translation of the draft National Technical Regulations (NTR) on the
phytosanitary requirements for imported regulated articles, proposed by the Ministry of Agriculture
and Rural Development’s Plant Protection Department. The objective of the NTR is to regulate the
quarantine requirements of imported plants. The draft NTR sets a zero tolerance on all quarantine
pests regulated under Circular 35/2014/TT-BNNPTNT issued in 2014. Vietnam has notified these
measures to the WTO as VNM112, allowing trading partners and stakeholders to comment before May
17, 2020.
