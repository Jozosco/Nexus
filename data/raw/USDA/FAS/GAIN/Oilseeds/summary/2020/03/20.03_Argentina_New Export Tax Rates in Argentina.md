Report Highlights:
On Wednesday, March 4, 2020 the Government of Argentina announced adjustments to its export tax
regime. Key modifications to existing rates included an increase from 30% to 33% for soybeans and
soybean products. Wheat, corn, and sorghum export taxes remain at 12%. Other commodities saw
reduction or maintenance of their current export tax rates.
