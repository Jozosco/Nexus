Report Highlights:
On March 31, 2020, the Eurasian Economic Commission (EEC), which is the regulatory body of the
Armenia-Belarus-Kazakhstan-Kyrgyzstan-Russia Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU), issued EEC Collegium
Decision No. 43, introducing a ban on exports from the EAEU of a number of food products, most
notably sunflower seeds and soy beans, as part of the EAEU coronavirus pandemic response. The
export ban will come into effect on April 12, 2020, and will last through June 30, 2020. This report
contains an unofficial English translation of the measure.
