Report Highlights:
In 2020, Vietnam had to confront dual COVID-19 and African Swine Fever outbreaks, which affected
both animal feed demand and domestic oil consumption. Closures of restaurants, hotels, schools, and
industrial canteens led to lower soybean consumption for food use and changed oil consumption habits.
Recovery in industrial-scale swine facilities and poultry and aquaculture production growth are expected
to increase soybean meal feed consumption in MY2020/21.
