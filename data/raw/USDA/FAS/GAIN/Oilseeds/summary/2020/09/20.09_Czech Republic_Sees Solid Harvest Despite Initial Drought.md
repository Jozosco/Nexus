Report Highlights:
The harvest of grains and rapeseed in the Czech Republic ended in the first week of September.
Estimates are optimistic that the production of grains will reach above the 10-year average. The Czech
Statistical Office estimates the average yield of grains (excluding corn) at 5.63 MT/HA and rapeseed
yield at 3.11 MT/HA, while the Czech Ministry of Agriculture reports grain yield (excl. corn) at 6.06
MT/HA and rapeseed 3.42 MT/HA, based on harvest monitoring.
