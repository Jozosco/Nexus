Report Highlights:
EU oilseeds production in MY 2020/21 is characterized by the lowest rapeseed production in more than
a decade. Total major oilseeds (rapeseed, sunflower, soybeans) production is forecast to decline by
about 1.5 percent year-on-year. The Covid-19 pandemic and its lockdown measures are expected to
have a negative impact on oilseeds meal use in feed ratios due to lower meat consumption.
