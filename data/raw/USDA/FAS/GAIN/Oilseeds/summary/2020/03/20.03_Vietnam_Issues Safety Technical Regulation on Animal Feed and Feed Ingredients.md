Report Highlights:
This report provides an unofficial translation of Circular 4/2020 dated March 9, 2020 of the Ministry of
Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD) to issue National Technical Regulation (NTR) 190:2020 –
Maximum allowable levels of safety indicators in animal feed and feed ingredients. This Technical
Regulation renews tolerances and maximum levels of micro-organisms, heavy metals, and mycotoxins
in feed and feed ingredients. This Technical Regulation shall apply from July 1, 2020, for quality
examinations for imported animal feed and feed ingredients in Vietnam.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
