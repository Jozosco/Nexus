On June 2, 2020, the Eurasian Economic Commission (EEC), which is the regulatory body of the
Armenia-Belarus-Kazakhstan-Kyrgyzstan-Russia Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU), issued EEC
Collegium Decision No. 71, amending its temporary ban on exports of some food products from the
EAEU to exclude soybeans from the ban. The amendment comes into effect on June 13, 2020, and
allows exports of soybeans from the EAEU as of June 1, 2020. This report contains an unofficial
English translation of the measure.
