Report Highlights:
Post forecasts that year-on-year Romanian oilseed production will decline by 10.7 percent in marketing
year (MY) 2020/21, mainly due to a decline in the sunflower area. Oilseed exports are projected to
decline by 16 percent. Post expects lower Romanian rapeseed yields for MY 2020/21 due to dry
planting conditions and inconsistent spring weather. Conversely, early May rains provided a needed
boost to the sunflower and soybean crops.
