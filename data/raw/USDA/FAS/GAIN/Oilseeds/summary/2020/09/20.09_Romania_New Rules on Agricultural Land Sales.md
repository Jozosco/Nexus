Report Highlights:
In August 2020, Romania amended its agricultural land sale law following almost two years of debate in
the Romanian Parliament. Law 175/2020 is scheduled to enter into force in mid-October 2020. It
restricts non-Romanian farmland ownership and penalizes land owners selling farmland prior to eight
years after the purchase date.

Summary of the New Rules
In early June 2020, the Romanian Parliament approved a new draft law regarding agricultural land sales
following two years of parliamentary debate. After a politically-charged legal challenge to the draft,
Romania’s Constitutional Court ruled in mid-July 2020 that the draft was constitutional. In mid-August
2020, Romania’s President signed Law 175/2020, which defines preemption right holders, their order,
and the conditions for natural persons or companies to acquire agricultural land. Regarding preemption
right holders, first-degree preemptors are co-owners, immediate family and spouses, and other relatives.
