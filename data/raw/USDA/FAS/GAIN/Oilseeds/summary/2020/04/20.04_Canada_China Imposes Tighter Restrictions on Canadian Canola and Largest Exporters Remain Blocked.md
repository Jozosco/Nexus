Report Highlights:
China continues to allow imports of Canadian canola seed though China recently reduced the
acceptable dockage limits on imported canola and did not reinstate the licenses of Canada’s two
largest Canola exporters.
On March 30, 2020, China lowered the dockage, or foreign material, limits on Canadian canola seed imports
from 2.5 to 1 percent which increases the processing cost for Canadian canola seed shippers. The new dockage
requirement is likely to reduce Canadian canola seed exports to China even further, which have decreased 70
percent through the first half of the 2019/20 marketing year.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
