Report Highlights:
In mid-April, the Government of Romania (GOR) approved Emergency Ordinance (E.O.) 47/2020 to
provide flexibility from biofuel mandates for local petroleum stakeholders. The GOR also subsequently
passed implementing regulation Ministerial Order 890/2020 which outlines how Romanian fuel
suppliers can seek exemptions from GOR biofuel blending mandates during the ongoing state of
emergency. While locally produced biodiesel can cover roughly 70 percent of domestic demand,
Romania is fully dependent on imported bioethanol.
