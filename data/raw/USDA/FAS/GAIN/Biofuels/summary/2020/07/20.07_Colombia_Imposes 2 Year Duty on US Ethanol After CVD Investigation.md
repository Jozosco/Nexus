Report Highlights:
On May 7, 2020, the Colombian Ministry of Trade, Industry and Tourism (MinCIT) issued a final ruling
in its countervailing duty (CVD) investigation into U.S. ethanol, placing a $0.066/kg duty on imported
U.S. ethanol for the next two years as compensation. This report summarizes the Colombian ethanol
market and the CVD case on U.S. ethanol.
