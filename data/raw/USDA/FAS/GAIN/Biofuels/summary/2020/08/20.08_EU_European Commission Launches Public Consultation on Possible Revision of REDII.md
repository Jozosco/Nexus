On August 4, 2020, the European Commission launched a public consultation on the potential revision
of the 2018 Renewable Energy Directive (REDII). The Commission will conduct an impact assessment
to evaluate if REDII needs to be amended in order to better align it with the EU’s new Green Deal and
its principle goal of making the EU climate neutral by 2050. Stakeholders have until September 21,
2020 to comment.
