General Information:
I. Executive Summary
Long term goals of the Czech energy strategy as defined in the State Energy Conception of the Czech
Republic 2014 are: safety, competitiveness and sustainability. The Czech biofuel policy reflects that,
being also significantly influenced by the European Union policy and regulations. Major energy
sources and percent share of total Czech electricity generation based on installed capacities in 2012 are:
coal- 52 percent, nuclear – 20 percent, hydropower 11 percent, solar – 10 percent, natural gas – 6
percent, wind – 1 percent.
