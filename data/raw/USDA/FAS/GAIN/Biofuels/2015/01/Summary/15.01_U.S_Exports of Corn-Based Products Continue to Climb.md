U.S. exports of corn-based products have experienced rapid growth in the past decade, reaching
a value of $7.6 billion in fiscal year 2014, up 16 percent from the previous year. Exports of U.S.

importance for the U.S. corn industry. In fact, in FY 2013, the value of these products surpassed
the value of bulk corn exports (although bulk exports recovered in FY 2014).
