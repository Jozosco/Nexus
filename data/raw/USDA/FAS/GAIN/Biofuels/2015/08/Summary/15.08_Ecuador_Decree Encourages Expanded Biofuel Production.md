Report Highlights:
On May 13, 2015, Ecuador President Rafael Correa signed Executive Decree 675. The decree doubles
to ten percent the ethanol blend rate in Ecuadorian regular grade (87-octane) gasoline. The decree
establishes that the price of a liter of ethanol is no longer pegged to raw sugar prices as set by the
Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) in New York. Rather prices are calculated as a function of the average
market price of ethanol along the U.S. Gulf Coast. For blended fuels to account for 45 percent of the
market by 2017, Ecuador will need to plant an additional 30,000 hectares of sugarcane.
General Information:
On May 13, 2015, Ecuador President Rafael Correa signed Executive Decree 675. The decree doubles
to ten percent the bioethanol (ethanol) blend rate in Ecuadorian regular grade (87-octane) gasoline. This
decree became effective June 1, 2015, following its publication in Ecuador’s official gazette
(Suplemento 512). FAS Quito understands that ethanol will be distilled from sugarcane.
