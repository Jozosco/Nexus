Japan’s Agency of Natural Resources and Energy will accept public comments on its proposed 6th

Japanese.
THIS REPORT CONTAINS ASSESSMENTS OF COMMODITY AND TRADE ISSUES MADE BY USDA STAFF
AND NOT NECESSARILY STATEMENTS OF OFFICIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT POLICY
Background
On July 21, 2021, the Agency of Natural Resources and Energy (ANRE) of the Ministry of Economy,
Trade and Industry (METI) released Japan’s draft 6th Strategic Energy Plan (SEP) (for details, see
JA2021-0113). Since then, ANRE made several minor revisions following meetings of the expert
committee. On September 3, 2021, ANRE opened the comment period for the proposed SEP to the
public. The Japanese Cabinet will vote on the 6th SEP following the conclusion of the public comment
period.
Process for Comment Submission:
Comments must be (i) submitted in Japanese by October 4, 2021 (Japan Standard time) and (ii) specific
and refer to the relevant page number and line number of the draft SEP. Comments can be submitted
