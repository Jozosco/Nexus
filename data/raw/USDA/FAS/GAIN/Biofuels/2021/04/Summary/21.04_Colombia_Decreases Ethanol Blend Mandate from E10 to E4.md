Report Highlights:
On March 31, 2021, the Government of Colombia (GOC) issued a joint resolution to decrease the
ethanol blend mandate from 10 percent (E10) to 4 percent (E4) starting on April 1, 2021. The measure
establishes that the mandatory blend rate will gradually return to E10 in September 2021. The measure
attributes the blend mandate change to inclement weather that impacted domestic production as early as
January 2021. It also attributes this change to increasing U.S. ethanol prices that discouraged imports.
