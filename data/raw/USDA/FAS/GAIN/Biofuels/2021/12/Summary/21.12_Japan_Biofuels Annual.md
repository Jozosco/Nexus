Report Highlights:
FAS/Tokyo expects Japan’s imports of bioethanol to stay steady in line with Japan’s long-standing
annual target for on-road biofuel of 500 million liters crude oil equivalent which is entirely fulfilled by
bioETBE. There is no national program for biodiesel, and it is therefore in effect absent from the market
except in a few localized cases. Japan has yet to realize a market for sustainable aviation fuel (SAF),
although sustained movement in this direction is now evident. As for the use of biomass in stationary
power applications, Japan will see an increase in wood pellet imports as new large biomass power
plants, supported by a feed-in tariff, become operational.
