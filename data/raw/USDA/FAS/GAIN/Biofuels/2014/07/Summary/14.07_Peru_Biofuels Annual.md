GAIN Report Number:
Peru
Biofuels Annual 2014
Peru's Ethanol Production Up, Biodiesel However Tumbles
Approved By:
Mariano J. Beillard
Prepared By:
Gaspar E. Nolte and Mariano J. Beillard
Report Highlights:
FAS Lima forecasts Peru’s calendar (CY) 2015 ethanol production at about 250 million liters, up by 5
million liters or two percent compared to our CY 2014 estimate of 245 million liters. We forecast
domestic ethanol consumption at 165 million liters in CY 2015, up 35 million liters or 27 percent
compared to our 2014 estimate of 130 million liters. We foresee Peruvian CY 2015 ethanol exports at
about 160 million liters, up by 5 million liters or over 3 percent compared to our CY 2014 estimate of
155 million liters. Peru will not produce biodiesel in CY 2014, nor in 2015 due to the availability of
more affordable Argentine biodiesel. We forecast CY 2015 biodiesel imports to remain flat at 283,000
metric tons.
Executive Summary:
FAS Lima forecasts Peru’s calendar year (CY) 2015 ethanol production at about 250 million liters, up
by 5 million liters or two percent compared to our CY 2014 estimate of 245 million liters. We forecast
domestic ethanol consumption at 165 million liters in CY 2015, up 35 million liters or 27 percent
compared to our 2014 estimate of 130 million liters.
