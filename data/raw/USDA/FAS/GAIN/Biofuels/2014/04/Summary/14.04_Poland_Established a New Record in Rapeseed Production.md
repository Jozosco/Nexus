Report Highlights:
In MY 2013/14, Poland’s rapeseed planted area reached one million HA and produced a record crop of
2.8 million tons. Both a large increase in acreage and higher yields contributed to the crop’s success.
For MY 2014/15, planted area is expected to diminish 13 percent in response to declining rapeseed
prices leading some producers to switch to grains. As of the first week of April, the rapeseed crop was
reported behind in development as compared to the same period 2013.
