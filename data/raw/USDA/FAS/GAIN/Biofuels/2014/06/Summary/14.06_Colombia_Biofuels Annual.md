The Government of Colombia (GOC) has not communicated a clear policy vision for the biofuels

2015. Current mandates remain unchanged for 2014 resulting in little stimulation in production or
consumption patterns. A U.S. ethanol export surge has motivated the Colombian cane sugar industry to
seek greater trade protection, resulting in the passage of a GOC resolution that establishes policy
conditions to implement non-tariff barriers to trade.
