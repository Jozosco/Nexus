Report Highlights:
As one of the world's leading producers and exporters of oil and gas, biofuels have an insignificant
share in the overall energy production matrix of Russia, with an estimate of only 1.2 percent, and
biomass accounting for only 0.5 percent. Russia is unlikely to develop ethanol for fuel until the GOR
has taken serious steps in supporting the industry by developing the regulatory base and policy measures
to support local producers. Wood pellet production and exports will likely continue to grow, driven
primarily by increasing demand from Europe.
