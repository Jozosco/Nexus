Report Highlights:
The Italian biofuels industry is slowly developing to meet the EU’s 2020 mandatory 10-percent
biofuel use in transportation fuels. However, lack of support from the government, stiff
competition from South America, and a complicated and uncertain EU and Italian legislative
framework are severely hampering the industry’s growth. Italian biodiesel output is expected to
fall some 32 percent to about 500,000 MT in 2011 while the bioethanol fuel production is still not
relevant.
General Information:
Policy and programs

31 with the 55/2011 Decree. According to the Italian legislative practice, any provision contained
in these decrees has to be implemented through another specific decree. All the provisions
