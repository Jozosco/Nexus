Report Highlights:
The new Salvadoran government (GOES) headed by President Mauricio Funes, which took office on June 1, 2009, has not
approved the Ethanol Law that was created by the previous administration. The present administration has decided to change
the gasoline-sugarcane mix ratio to 95 to 5 percent instead of the proposed 90 to 10 percent by the previous administration.
This mixture does not affect normal gasoline engines avoiding the need for flex car engines. There are currently several bio-
fuel initiatives being implemented to produce bio-diesel from higuerillo (Ricinus communis) and tempate (Jatropha curcas)
plants, as well as ethanol from imported dehydrated Brazilian alcohol to take advantage of a CAFTA-DR quota. However,
high international sugarcane prices have decreased the interest on part of the sugarcane industry to lobby for the ethanol law.
The U.S. continues supporting El Salvador in identifying alternative renewable energies and has recently provided
$250,000.00 under the Alliance for the Americas for Energy and Climate.
