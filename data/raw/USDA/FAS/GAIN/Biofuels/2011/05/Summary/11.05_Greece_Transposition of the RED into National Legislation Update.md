Greece has not yet implemented the EU’s Renewable Energy Directive (RED) 2009/28/EC. The Ministry for
Environment, Energy and Climate Change established a Working Group to draft implementing regulations.
Introduction
Renewable energy is crucial to any move towards a low carbon economy. It is also a key
component of the EU-27 energy strategy, developing a diverse range of mostly indigenous energy
resources.
EU renewable energy policy is relatively young, having started with the adoption of the 1997 White
Paper. It has been driven by the need to de-carbonise the energy sector and address growing
dependency on fossil fuel imports from politically unstable regions outside the EU. Over that
period, the focus has shifted from the promotion of renewable energy through indicative targets for
the electricity and transport sectors to the definition of legally binding targets supported by a
comprehensive legislative framework, and most recently, by a reorientation of European energy
infrastructure policy that facilitates renewable energy growth.
