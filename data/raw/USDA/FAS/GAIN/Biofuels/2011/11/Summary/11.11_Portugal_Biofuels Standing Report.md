Report Highlights:
There is no production of bioethanol for transport fuel in Portugal. Biodiesel production is
steady at approximately 350,000 MT under a quota system. The Portuguese biodiesel industry is
favorable to the use of soybean oil and to further soybean imports. Portugal is advocating for a
method to determine energy and emission credits for co-products that does not discriminate
against soybeans.
