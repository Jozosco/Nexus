Report Highlights:
Greece has not yet implemented the new Renewable Energy Directive (RED) 2009/28/EC. The Ministry for
Environment, Energy, and Climate Change set up a Working Group to draft implementing legislation. The draft
bill is currently ready and will be uploaded online for public consultation (which is expected to last
approximately 10 days). The working group will then incorporate the comments of the public consultation to the
draft bill by Jan. 31st, 2012.
