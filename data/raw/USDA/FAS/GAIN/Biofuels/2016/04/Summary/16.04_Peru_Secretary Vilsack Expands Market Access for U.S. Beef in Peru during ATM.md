Report Highlights:
Secretary of Agriculture Thomas Vilsack’s March 13-16 visit to Lima invigorated the $3 billion U.S.-
Peru bilateral agricultural trade relationship, reopening the Peruvian market to all U.S. beef and beef
products. His trip to Peru, the first by a sitting Secretary of Agriculture and one of the longest in
Secretary Vilsack’s tenure, highlighted the remarkable success and transformative impact of the U.S.-
Peru Trade Promotion Agreement (PTPA). Secretary Vilsack highlighted the benefits of the Trans-
Pacific Partnership (TPP), cooperation on climate change, and opportunities for bilateral ethanol trade.
He underscored with interlocutors and local press how Peru’s trade-driven economic growth has been a
driver for reducing poverty. A 34-company strong agribusiness trade mission, one of the largest to date
for USDA, accompanied the Secretary.
