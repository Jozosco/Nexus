Canada’s Clean Fuels Standards Delayed
On April 24, 2020, Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) announced a six-month delay to the
publication of the proposed Clean Fuel Standard regulations for liquid fuels in Canada Gazette, Part I. The delay
results from the unprecedented circumstances Canada is facing as a result of COVID-19. ECCC now plans to
publish the proposed regulations in fall 2020, finalize regulations in late 2021 and begin enforcing regulations in
mid-2022. Regulations for the gaseous and solids classes are expected to come into force in 2023.
The new timeline as revised on April 24, 2020 is as follows:
 Spring/summer 2020: Engagement with the Technical Working Group on key regulatory design
elements.
