Background
In an effort to limit sugar production and increase domestic production of ethanol, the CCEA, chaired by the
Prime Minister of India approved following prices for the forthcoming sugar season 2020/21 during the ethanol
supply period, which runs from December 1, 2020 to November 30, 2021:
 Ethanol derived from C-heavy molasses fixed at INR 45.69/liter or $0.61/liter (from the previous price
of INR 43.75/liter).
 Ethanol derived out of B-heavy molasses and partial sugarcane juice fixed at INR 57.61/liter or
$0.77/liter (from the previous price of INR 54.27 per liter).
