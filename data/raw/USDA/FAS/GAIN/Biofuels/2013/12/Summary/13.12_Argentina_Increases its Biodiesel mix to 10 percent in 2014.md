Report Highlights:
The Argentine Government raises the biodiesel mix under the local mandate from 8 to 10 percent in 2014 to
partially offset smaller exports due to market restrictions imposed by the EU.
General Information:
On December 2, 2013 the Argentine government announced that the mix of biodiesel with diesel will increase
from the current 8 percent to 9 percent in January 2014 and to 10 percent in February 2014. It will also oblige
thermo-electric plants to use the same mix. This measure is a way of compensating the local biodiesel industry
which has seen a significant cut in exports to the EU, its number one market. In late November, the EU
implemented an average countervailing duty of 24.6 percent on biodiesel imports from Argentina due to alleged
dumping. This measure is expected to remain in place for the next five years. Argentina lodged a formal
complaint at the WTO over the duties, and the Argentine biofuels chamber, CARBIO, said last week that it plans
