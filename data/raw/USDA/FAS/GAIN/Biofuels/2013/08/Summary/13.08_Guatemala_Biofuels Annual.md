Report Highlights:
Guatemala has no law in place to promote biofuels development. Guatemala’s installed capacity since
2011 continues to be 269 million liters of alcohol from sugarcane byproducts, of which less than 70
million liters corresponds to dehydrated ethanol. Ethanol as a biofuel continues to be mainly exported
to Europe. Biodiesel production is extremely limited, reaching less than 1 million liter per year. A
proposed mix of 10 percent ethanol to gasoline for domestic consumption could reduce the import bill
for petroleum by US$330 million and would generate employment opportunities – but no such
regulation is likely to be considered in the near term.

Guatemala City
