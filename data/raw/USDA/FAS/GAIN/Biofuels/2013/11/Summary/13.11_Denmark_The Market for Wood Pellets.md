Denmark is the number one ranking importer of wood pellets in the world.
In Denmark, wood pellets are being used in small boilers in private homes, medium sized district heating plants
and in large combined heat and power (CHP) plants. Since 2010, the main driver of growth has been large scale
CHP plants that have been replacing coal. Current use is estimated at about 1 MMT. Since 2000, Danish
production of wood pellets has been fluctuating between 130,000 and 200,000 MT annually and is to a large
extent based on residues from the furniture and wood processing industry. Due to the limited domestic
