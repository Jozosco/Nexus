Executive Summary:
In 2013, ethanol consumption will likely double to 800 million liters or approximately 2.2 million liters
per day compared to 1.1 million liters in 2012. The expected increase is attributed to an increase in E10
(a mixture of 10 percent ethanol and 90 percent gasoline) and E20 (a mixture of 20 percent ethanol and
80 percent gasoline) gasohol consumption driven by the government’s decision to phase out the use of
Octane 91 regular gasoline. The elimination of Octane 91 has increased the use of E10 and E20 gasohol
to 20 million liters per day compared to 12.2 million liters per day in 2012.
