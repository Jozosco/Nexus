General Information:
The transposition of the Renewable Directive, 28/2009/EC, into Regulations of the Republic of Malta
was done by establishing new Regulations and by amendments to a series of others. The new
Regulations are:
(i) Promotion of Energy from Renewable Energy Sources Regulations (Legal Notice 538 of 2010 as
amended by Legal Notice 2010 of 2012);
(ii) The Biofuels (Sustainability Criteria) Regulations (Legal Notice 553 of 2010 as amended by Legal
Notice 50 of 2012); and
(iii) The Biofuels and Bioliquids Market Regulations (Legal Notice 85 of 2012 as amended by Legal
Notice 184 of 2012).
The following regulations were amended to include provisions relating to the Renewable Energy
Market:
(i) Legal Notice 68 of 2011 amending Legal Notice 278 of 2007 –
