Report Highlights:
The 2016 elimination of the bioethanol specific target resulted in reduced market opportunities for
bioethanol producers. Also, lower domestic bioethanol production since 2016 has opened up new
opportunities for DDG imports. We expect little or no impact on raw material sourcing when
sustainability measures are fully in place in January 2018, as producers are already using certified
feedstocks.
