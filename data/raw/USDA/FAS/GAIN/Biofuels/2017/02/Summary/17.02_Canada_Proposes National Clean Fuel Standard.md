Canada Proposes National Clean Fuel Standard
Environment and Climate Change Canada is consulting with provinces, territories and stakeholders to
develop a regulation under the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999 to reduce Canada’s
greenhouse gas emissions (GHG) through the increased use of lower carbon fuels and alternative
technologies. The purpose of the Clean Fuel Standard Discussion Paper is to facilitate consultations to
help inform the development of the regulatory framework in advance of developing a Clean Fuel
Standard.
