Report Highlights:
As part of the ambitious goals Brazil committed to at the 21st Conference of the Parties (COP21) of
the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change, the Ministry of Mines and Energy
announced the RenovaBio program in December 2016. The program proposes to create a
regulatory framework to revitalize the biofuels sector, encouraging energy efficiency gains in
biofuels production and use, and will recognize that different biofuels have different capacities to
contribute to the de-carbonization goals set at COP21. In August 2017, the Brazilian Government
put a tariff rate quota in place for ethanol imports, allowing 600 million liters to enter duty free,
with any volume above being subject to a 20% tariff. This followed a March 2017 request by
Brazilian ethanol producers to place a tariff on ethanol imports. Producers claim the pace of
