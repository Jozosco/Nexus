Report Highlights:
On May 24, 2019, the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce
and Industry issued a notification that biofuel imports (non-fuel use) in India will now require an import
license, subject to actual user conditions. The previous policy condition did not require an import
license.
General Information:
On May 24, 2019, the DGFT issued a notification, requiring Indian importers of biofuels (non-fuel use)
(HS Code 2207 2000, 2710 2000, 3826 0000) to obtain an import license, subject to actual user
conditions. Biofuel imports for fuel use remain ‘restricted’. Note: the notification will come into effect
on the date it is published in the official Gazette of India.
Background
The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India (GOI) formally notified the National
Biofuel Policy (NBP) 2018 on June 4, 2018. Target of 20 percent blending of ethanol with gasoline, and
5 percent blending of biodiesel with diesel by 2030, were proposed.
