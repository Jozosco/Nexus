The Department of Agriculture (DA) and the Department of Energy (DOE) continue to collaborate on

programs aimed at boosting the country’s energy and food security. Biofuels – bioethanol and biodiesel

specific targets for bioethanol and biofuels, specifically on feedstocks and their contribution to
environmental protection.
