General Information
Japan’s “Act on Promotion of Use of Non-Fossil Energy Sources and Effective Use of Fossil Energy
Raw Materials by Energy Suppliers” (hereafter referred to as the “Sophisticated Act”) sets Japan’s
biofuel policy. The Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) oversees the implementation of
the Sophisticated Act, as well as related rules and standards.
On September 2, 2022, METI’s Agency for Natural Resources and Energy (ANRE) held “the 6th
Technical Review Committee Meeting toward the Introduction of Biofuels in Japan” (meeting notes
available in Japanese only). This expert committee meeting kicked off ANRE’s public-facing process of
