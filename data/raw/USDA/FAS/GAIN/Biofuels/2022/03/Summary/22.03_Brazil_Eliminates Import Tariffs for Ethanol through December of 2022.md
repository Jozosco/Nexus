Report Highlights:
On Monday, March 21st, the Government of Brazil (GOB) decided to temporarily eliminate the import
tariff of ethanol, as well as five other agricultural products (coffee, margarine, cheese, spaghetti, sugar,
and soybean oil). The decision was made at a meeting of the Executive Management Committee
(GECEX) of the Brazilian Chamber of Foreign Trade (CAMEX), in order to help mediate rising
gasoline prices. Zeroing the tariff should benefit exports of U.S. ethanol to Brazil, which have been
negatively impacted by tariffs since 2017.
