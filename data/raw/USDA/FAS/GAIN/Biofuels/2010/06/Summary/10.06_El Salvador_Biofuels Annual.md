Report Highlights:
The new Salvadoran government (GOES) headed by President Mauricio Funes, which took office on June 1, 2009 has
not approved the Ethanol Law that was created by the previous administration. Under this law, gasoline would be
required to be mixed with sugarcane-derived ethanol on a 90%-10% ratio. This mixture does not affect normal gasoline
engines avoiding the need for flex car engines. Currently ,there are several bio-fuel initiatives being implemented to
produce bio-diesel from higuerillo (Ricinus communis) and tempate (Jatropha curcas) plants, and ethanol from imported
dehydrated Brazilian alcohol to take advantage of a CAFTA-DR quota. However, the reduction in oil prices has
decreased the interest in new sources of energy for the moment. The U.S. continues supporting El Salvador in
identifying alternative renewable energies and has recently provided $250,000.00 under the Alliance for the Americas for
Energy and Climate.

San Salvador
