Report Highlights:
Under Peru's biofuels legislation, beginning in CY 2011, gasoline must contain 7 percent ethanol
and diesel must include 5 percent biodiesel. Ethanol production in CY 2011 is estimated to reach
130 million liters. Peru's biodiesel consumption in CY 2011 is estimated at 227,000 MT. U.S.
biodiesel exports to Peru are assessed a countervailing duty of $178 per MT and an anti-dumping
duty of $212 per MT.
Post:
Lima
Executive Summary:
Production of ethanol for fuel in Peru began in August 2009. Currently there is one operation up
and running and another one in a pre-operation phase. Both of these operations are in the
northern region of Piura. Ethanol in Peru is produced out of sugar cane. High yields and year
round harvest grants Peru's ethanol producing companies a competitive advantage when compared
to other producers in the region. Ethanol consumption is estimated at 30 million cubic meters in
CY 2010. However, once the ethanol blending schedule reaches the whole country in CY 2011,
consumption is estimated to reach 80 million cubic meters.
