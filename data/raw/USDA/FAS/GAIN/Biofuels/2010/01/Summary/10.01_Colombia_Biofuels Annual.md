Report Highlights:
In 2010, ethanol production is estimated to drop to 280 million liters due to a dramatic reduction in
the sugarcane supply caused by extreme rainfall at the end of 2010. Production is expected to recover to
300 million liters in 2011, as weather improves, with production capacity increasing 100,000 liters per
day in 2011. A new ethanol project entered into production in 2010 using cassava as feedstock. A
new government regulation states that starting July 2010, new vehicles for public transportation will use
more environmentally friendly fuels and new private vehicles must possess flex-fuel technology by
2012.
