Report Highlights:
This report provides a brief analysis of EU Member State (MS) national renewable energy action plans, which are part of a
broad EU effort to increase the use of biofuels in transportation and electricity production. The report also considers the
implications for domestic crop production and trade. For example, to meet the EU’s stated goals, it is estimated that an
additional 10 MMT of grains and 12 MMT of oils and fats may be required annually.
