The Hungarian Parliament passed the legislation titled “Promoting the Use of Renewable Energy and
the Reduction of Greenhouse Gas Emission of Energy Used in Transport” on November 2, 2010.
General Information:
The Ministry of National Development recently submitted legislation to Parliament promoting the use
of bio-fuels in Hungary. The bill is titled, “Promoting the Use of Renewable Energy and the Reduction
of Greenhouse Gas Emission of Energy Used in Transport”. On November 2, the Hungarian Parliament
passed the bill, in time to meet the December 5 deadline for member state implementation of EU-wide
renewable fuels legislation. However, implementing regulations are still needed in Hungary. Ministry-
level directives will be written to further define the default values (relating to green house gasses and
sustainability) and the exact definitions of the biofuels covered. The Ministerial executive orders are
expected to be completed during the first quarter of 2011.
