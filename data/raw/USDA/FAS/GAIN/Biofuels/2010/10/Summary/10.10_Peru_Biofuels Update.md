Report Highlights:
Peru delays mandatory inclusion of ethanol in gasoline until June 1, 2011
General Information:
On September 28, 2010, the government of Peru published Supreme Decree 061-2010-EM establishing a new schedule for
enforcing the 7.8 percent ethanol inclusion in gasoline. The highlight of this new schedule is that it postpones until June 1,
2011 mandatory ethanol inclusion in the Region of Lima which is about 60 percent of the market. Originally Lima was
supposed to come on line in January 2010, and then it was delayed to October 1, 2010.
Apparently this Decree was an initiative of Minister of Environment Brack who believes there is not enough local ethanol
production to meet demand and does not want to force imports. However, producers have argued that there is a plenty of
local production and meeting the rising demand will not be a problem. Moreover, the Peruvian business association
(Sociedad Nacional de Industrias) have questioned Brack’s proposal, explaining that this measure would delay already
planned investment valued at around $1 billion.
