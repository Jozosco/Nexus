Report Highlights:
The Government of India (GOI) approved the National Policy on Biofuels on December 24, 2009. The policy proposes a
target of 20 percent blending of bio-diesel and bio-ethanol by 2017. India’s biofuel strategy continues to focus on the
use of non-food resources; namely sugar molasses for production of ethanol and non-edible oils for the production of
biodiesel. The government’s current target of 5 percent blending of ethanol in petrol has been successful in years of
surplus sugar production, but unfilled when sugar production declines. Biodiesel production in India is very small due to
inadequate supplies of feedstock.
