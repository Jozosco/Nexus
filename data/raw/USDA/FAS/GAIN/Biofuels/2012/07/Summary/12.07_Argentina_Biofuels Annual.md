Report Highlights:
Argentine biodiesel production in 2013 is projected at 2.8 billion liters, slightly lower than the record of 2012.
The closing of the Spanish market, Argentina’s top export destination, is forecast to hurt overall exports, set at
1.5 billion liters. Domestic consumption in 2013 is forecast to continue growing, but there is uncertainty if the
government will increase the mandate mix from 7 to 10 percent. Bioethanol production in 2013 is projected at
a record 400 million liters. The incorporation of five new plants using grains will drive up production capacity to
720 million liters by the end of 2013.

Buenos Aires

both biodiesel and bioethanol plants.
