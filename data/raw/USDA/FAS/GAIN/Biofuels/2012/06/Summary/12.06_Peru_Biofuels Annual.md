Executive Summary:
Ethanol production in CY 2012 is expected at 220 million liters, increasing 22 percent compared to the
previous year. There are two ethanol operations in Peru; both are in the northern region of Piura.
Ethanol in Peru is produced out of sugar cane. Peru’s favorable weather conditions and rich soil enable
a year-round-harvest of sugar cane with very high yields, up to 200 metric tons (MT) of sugar cane per
hectare (average yields are 160 MT per hectare). Ethanol consumption is forecasted at 85 million liters
in CY 2013.
