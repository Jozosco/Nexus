I. Executive Summary
Under Thailand’s ethanol policy, the government promotes the use of gasohol (ethanol-gasoline blends)
through price incentives (tax relief at the retail level) and an excise tax reduction for cars compatible
with E20 and E85 gasohol. To increase biodiesel consumption, the government requires the mandatory
blending of biodiesel in diesel supplied across all market sectors, including road use, trains, inland
shipping, agriculture, and industry.
