Report Highlights:
Ethanol production in Peru is expected to reach 161 million liters in 2018 falling two percent
compared to the previous year due to increased production of sugar. Ethanol imports in 2018 are
forecast at 200 million liters. Biodiesel production is forecast at 62 million liters in 2018 while
imports are forecast at 274 million liters. Peru created E7.8 and B5 mandates for ethanol and
biodiesel which were reached in 2013 and 2012, respectively. Blending has remained at that level
since with all further growth tied to increased fuel pool size.
Executive Summary:
Peru's Consumer Defense and Intellectual Protection Institute (INDECOPI) initiated a
countervailing duty investigation against U.S. ethanol exports to Peru on April 25, 2017.
INDECOPI held the final hearing of interested parties on September 27, 2018. At the time of
writing, a determination has been released, but due to the option to appeal, the case is ongoing and
there is no final outcome at this time. A final determination is expected by the end of 2018.
