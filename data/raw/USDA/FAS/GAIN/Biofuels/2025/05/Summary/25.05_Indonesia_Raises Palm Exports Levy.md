On May 14, 2025, Indonesia issued Ministry of Finance Regulation No. 30/2025 raising export
levies for most of its palm products. According to the decree, the levy adjustments are necessary
“to increase the productivity of plantation products and provide added value to downstream
products at the farm level.” The increases affect 34 palm products, including six additional
products that were not previously subject to levies, such glycerin, soap stocks, and refined palm
oil mill effluent (POME). The new levy rates took effect on May 17, 2025.
