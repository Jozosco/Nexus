Report Highlights:
On July 8, 2025, Ontario announced its decision to proceed with domestic bio-based content
requirements for both diesel fuel and gasoline in the Ontario market. At least 75 percent of the
renewable content required in diesel fuel and 64 percent of the renewable content required in gasoline
must be produced in Canada. This makes Ontario the second province in Canada to announce domestic
content requirements. According to Canadian industry, Ontario's decision to adopt the new rules was
motivated by the Canadian federal government’s nonresponse to the U.S. Clean Fuel Production Tax
Credit (45Z).
