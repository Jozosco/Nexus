Report Highlights:
Colombia’s biofuels consumption is expected to modestly rebound in 2025, driven by increased ethanol
and biodiesel output from improved crop yields and continued 10 percent blending mandates. Ethanol
consumption is forecast to reach 955 million liters (ML) and will be met nearly equally through local
production and U.S. imports. Biodiesel demand is anticipated to recover to 795 ML, supplied solely by
local production. Established more than 20 years ago, Colombia maintains a relatively mature biofuels
program, but the government has not unveiled any major policies in recent years. Colombia's efforts on
advanced fuels resulted in the January 2025 SAF Roadmap, aiming to produce 100 million gallons
(378.5 ML) of Sustainable Aviation Fuel by 2035 and 450 million (1.7 billion liters) by 2050. To date,
one company has conducted SAF production tests in Colombia.
