Report Highlights:
In 2025, the Netherlands imported $535 million of U.S. biofuels, solidifying its role as a key hub for
sustainable road, aviation, and shipping fuels. Starting in 2026, Dutch legislation imposes greenhouse
gas (GHG) reduction mandates for marine and inland bunker suppliers, aiming to meet strict emission
targets. While the shipping sector explores various alternatives, a significant shift toward biobased diesel
fuels is anticipated to meet these evolving requirements. These regulatory changes, particularly focusing
on waste-based biofuels in the Port of Rotterdam, are creating new market opportunities for U.S.
biofuels and as well as feedstock suppliers.
