Report Highlights:
This report was re-issued in February 2025 to capture revisions to 2024 U.S. renewable diesel import
data derived from the U.S. Energy Information Agency. Rising ethanol use and blending continued in
2025, supported by the carbon credit exchange and provincial policies. The same was true for renewable
diesel and biodiesel in 2024 and expected in 2025. Fuel ethanol consumption in 2025 is forecast to be 52
percent higher than 2021 levels (pre-CFR); biodiesel and renewable diesel are forecast to be 20 percent
and 275 percent higher, respectively. Canada’s third, and largest, renewable diesel plant began operation
in July 2025, bringing capacity up from 994 ML in 2024 to 2,380 ML. Renewable diesel exports to the
United States began in June 2024. There is significant uncertainty for Canada’s biofuel production and
international trade given imminent changes in U.S. and Canadian federal biofuel policies, and due to
provincial domestic content requirements introduced in 2025.
