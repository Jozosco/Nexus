Report Highlights:
Brazil is the world’s third-largest corn producer, surpassed only by the United States and China. Its
growing corn output has led to an increase of corn as a feedstock for ethanol production, which is more
cost-effective than sugarcane ethanol. Corn ethanol production also generates valuable co-products—
dried distillers grains (DDG) and dried distillers grains with solubles (DDGS)—which are high-protein
feed for livestock. Brazil’s DDG/DDGS production rose from 1.2 million tons in 2019/20 to 4.2 million
tons in 2024/25, a 256% percent increase, with about 79 percent consumed domestically. The
government and industry are working to expand export markets. In 2024, Brazil exported 791,000 tons
of DDGS to 21 countries, worth $118 million.
